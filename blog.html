Host

Complete Sitarlive Host Registration Guide

Becoming a Sitarlive host starts with downloading the official app from SitarliveOfficial.in. Tap the "Join as Host" button on our host page to start the Sitarlive app download automatically. Once downloaded, install the app on your Android device and open it.

Inside the app, complete your profile with accurate details and enter Agency Code C063 during sign-up. This links your account to our support team, so we can help you with any questions during your first live sessions. If you get stuck at any step, our WhatsApp support team is ready to help.

Agency

Sitarlive Agency Registration Guide

Becoming a Sitarlive agency partner begins with a conversation. Visit our agency page and tap "Join as Agency" to message our partnership team directly on WhatsApp. We will walk you through agency responsibilities, expectations, and how Agency Code C063 works for your future hosts.

Once you understand the process, you can start recruiting hosts, helping them download the Sitarlive app, and guiding them through registration. Building a successful agency takes consistency, so focus on supporting a small group of hosts well before scaling up.

App

Sitarlive App Download Guide

Always download the Sitarlive app from the official link provided on SitarliveOfficial.in to avoid fake or unsafe files. Clicking "Join as Host" anywhere on this website instantly starts the Sitarlive app download for Android devices.

After the download finishes, open your file manager, tap the APK file, and allow installation if prompted. Once installed, open the app, create your profile, and enter Agency Code C063 during registration for guided support from our team.

Host

How to Become a Sitarlive Host

Anyone with an Android smartphone, a stable internet connection, and a valid mobile number can apply to become a Sitarlive host. There is no need for prior streaming experience or an existing audience — you can start from zero and grow over time.

Download the app, register with Agency Code C063, and go live for the first time. Consistency matters more than perfection, so aim to stream regularly and engage genuinely with your viewers. Visit our host registration page for the full step-by-step guide.

Agency

Agency Growth Tips for Sitarlive Partners

Growing a successful Sitarlive agency starts with recruiting the right hosts — people who are enthusiastic, consistent, and open to feedback. Focus on quality onboarding rather than rushing to sign up as many hosts as possible.

Stay in regular contact with your team, celebrate their wins, and share simple tips on going live effectively. Over time, a well-supported team of hosts becomes the foundation for sustainable agency growth. Learn more on our agency partnership page.

Sitarlive Beginner's Guide

New to Sitarlive? The platform offers two main ways to get involved: as a host, going live to build an audience and earn rewards, or as an agency, recruiting and supporting a team of hosts.

Whichever path you choose, registration starts with downloading the official Sitarlive app and using Agency Code C063 during sign-up. Our team on WhatsApp is available to answer any beginner questions along the way.
