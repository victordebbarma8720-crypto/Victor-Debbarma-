How to Complete DarlingC Host Registration

DarlingC host registration follows a clear, step-by-step process. Applicants should prepare genuine personal details, recent profile photos and a self-recorded verification video before starting.

  1. Download the DarlingC app from an official or verified source.
  2. Register using a phone number or Google account.
  3. Select the correct gender.
  4. Complete username, location and date of birth.
  5. Create a secure password.
  6. Open the Host Privileges or V Host section.
  7. Enter the agency code.
  8. Complete face verification.
  9. Upload genuine profile photos.
  10. Upload a self-recorded verification video.
  11. Review the information and submit the application.

Registration steps may change after app updates. See the full DarlingC host registration guidance for more detail.

DarlingC Host Eligibility and Required Documents

DarlingC host applicants must generally be women aged 18 or above. Applicants should provide genuine personal information, recent profile photos and self-recorded verification videos.

  • At least 18 years old
  • Genuine personal details
  • Real, recent profile photos
  • Self-recorded verification video
  • Stable internet and a clear front camera
  • Availability for regular interaction
  • Compliance with DarlingC rules

Full details are available on the DarlingC host registration page.

How to Start a DarlingC Agency

A DarlingC app agency recruits, trains, supports and manages approved hosts. Agency approval is controlled by DarlingC or its authorized administrators.

  1. Contact an authorized DarlingC agency manager or administrator.
  2. Submit a real name, country and phone number.
  3. Explain recruitment experience.
  4. Share the expected number of host recruits.
  5. Provide required identity or business information.
  6. Review agency rules and commission conditions.
  7. Wait for official approval.
  8. Receive or create agency-dashboard access.
  9. Add an approved payment account and start recruiting.

See the full DarlingC agency registration guidance.

DarlingC Agency Requirements and Responsibilities

Starting a DarlingC agency may be suitable for people with experience in host recruitment, online team management, video-calling applications or digital communication.

Agency responsibilities include recruiting eligible hosts, checking application materials, helping with verification, training new hosts, monitoring performance, explaining settlement procedures, keeping payment records and reporting fraud.

A new agency may be expected to recruit more than 10 eligible hosts during its initial development stage. Read the full DarlingC agency requirements.

DarlingC App Download and Installation Guide

Always download the DarlingC app from an official or verified source. Avoid links shared by unverified individuals promising guaranteed approval or income.

  1. Download the DarlingC app from an official or verified source.
  2. Register using a phone number or Google account.
  3. Complete the basic profile details, including gender, username, location and date of birth.
  4. Create a secure password and enable notifications.
  5. Continue to host or agency registration if applicable.

How DarlingC Hosts Earn

DarlingC provides approved hosts with different earning opportunities according to current platform rules, host level and eligibility. Possible activities include private video calls, private audio calls, eligible chat messages, paid photos, paid videos, virtual gifts, user invitations, recharge-related rewards, daily tasks, weekly bonuses and platform rankings.

Income is not guaranteed and depends on host activity, user engagement, platform traffic, host level and policy compliance. See the full host earnings guidance.

DarlingC Host Levels Explained

DarlingC hosts may be placed into levels such as SSS, SS, S, A, B and C. Levels may depend on weekly diamonds, valid online hours, call duration, answer rate, user engagement, weekly performance and compliance.

Higher levels may offer improved visibility and earning opportunities, subject to current platform policy.

DarlingC Diamond Exchange Rate Guide

As a general guide, approximately 450 to 800 diamonds may equal USD 1. The actual rate may depend on host level, diamond type, income source and current platform policy. Invitation diamonds may use a separate rate.

Hosts should always confirm the current exchange rate inside the DarlingC app.

DarlingC Withdrawal Guide

Individual withdrawal methods may include EPay, Binance, GCash or local bank transfer, with an approximate minimum withdrawal of USD 20. Eligible hosts may generally submit one request every 24 hours.

Under agency settlement, eligible host income may be transferred to the agency leader, with an approximate minimum agency-wallet transfer of USD 10. Hosts should confirm the settlement method before joining an agency.

Common DarlingC Registration Problems

  • Using unclear or outdated profile photos
  • Submitting a verification video that is a screen recording or copied clip
  • Entering an incorrect or missing agency code
  • Providing inconsistent personal information
  • Submitting an application outside official review hours
  • Sending money to unknown individuals who promise guaranteed approval

Applicants should follow official guidance closely and avoid unofficial shortcuts.

DarlingC Host Rules and Safety Guidelines

Hosts should use genuine photos and videos, work from a private environment with their face clearly visible, protect their account information and maintain one approved account.

Prohibited activities include fake photos, copied videos, account sharing, sharing WhatsApp or Telegram details, requesting payment outside the app and using a prerecorded video as a live camera. Violations may result in income deductions, suspension, withdrawal restrictions or permanent account closure.

DarlingC Agency Commission Guide

Without sub-agencies, agency commission may range from 10% to 25% based on monthly team income. With sub-agencies, the main agency may earn an additional 5% to 15% depending on sub-agency income tiers.

Commission may be adjusted at the beginning of the following month according to eligible team income from the previous month, and rates may change after official policy updates. See the full DarlingC agency commission table.
