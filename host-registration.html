Chingari Host Program Explained

The Chingari Host Program enables individuals to go live on the Chingari platform, connect with audiences in real-time, and build their personal brand in India's growing live streaming and creator economy.

As a Chingari Host, you have the flexibility to host live rooms from home, set your own schedule, engage with your audience, and grow your community — all using just your smartphone.
