Cherry hosting

An Introduction to Cherry Hosting

Cherry hosts take calls with users on the Cherry app. Hosting is done from a smartphone, so you can work from home, but it rewards consistency: being online during busy hours, answering calls promptly and keeping conversations friendly and respectful.

Hosts usually join through an agency, which helps with registration, explains the rules and shares your schedule. We can guide you through Cherry host registration on WhatsApp.

Hosting may suit you if you…

  • Are comfortable talking with new people
  • Can be online during evening rush hours (IST)
  • Can keep a regular weekly schedule
  • Are willing to follow community guidelines strictly
Eligibility

Host Eligibility and Requirements

Commonly requested requirements. Confirm the current list with us before registering.

Personal requirements

  • You meet the Cherry app’s minimum age requirement (adults only)
  • You can complete the app’s identity or profile verification
  • You agree to Cherry’s terms and community guidelines

Setup requirements

  • A smartphone with the official Cherry app
  • A stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or reliable mobile data)
  • A quiet, well-lit place to take calls; headphones help
How it works

How Cherry Hosting Works

  1. Register

    Contact us on WhatsApp, install the official Cherry app and complete registration and verification.

  2. Get your schedule

    Receive your assigned schedule, including hours in the rush-hour window.

  3. Take calls

    Go online during your hours, answer incoming calls promptly and keep conversations engaging.

  4. Track your targets

    Monitor your answer rate, average call duration and attendance against the plan conditions.

Performance

Performance Requirements

The supplied plan sets four performance conditions. Here is what each one means in practice.

🌙

3–5 hours in rush hour

Be online for 3–5 hours between 9 PM and 2 AM IST, when most calls are expected.

📞

Answer rate above 90%

Answer more than 9 out of every 10 incoming calls while you are online.

⏱️

Average call above 80 seconds

Keep your average call length above 80 seconds by being welcoming and engaged.

🗓️

Scheduled attendance

Log in on the days and hours assigned to you, and tell your agency early if you cannot.

FAQ

Cherry Host Registration FAQs

Who can become a Cherry host?

Adults who meet the Cherry app’s age and verification rules and can commit to a regular calling schedule. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm the current requirements.

Is the Cherry weekly base salary guaranteed?

No. The plan supplied to us lists a weekly base salary of $21–$35 for hosts who meet its performance conditions. Eligibility, amounts and rules can change and must be confirmed before you rely on them.

What are the rush hours for Cherry hosts?

In the plan supplied to us, rush hours are 9 PM to 2 AM India Standard Time (IST), and hosts are expected to be online for 3–5 hours during that window.

What answer rate and call duration are required?

The supplied plan lists a call answer rate above 90% and an average call duration above 80 seconds, along with attendance according to your assigned schedule.

Do I need special equipment to host?

A smartphone with the official Cherry app, a stable internet connection, a quiet and well-lit place and headphones are usually enough to get started.

Can I host part-time?

Hosting can fit around other commitments, but the salary plan supplied to us expects 3–5 hours during rush hours and attendance according to your assigned schedule.
