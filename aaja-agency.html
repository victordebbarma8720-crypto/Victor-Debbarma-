Overview

Aaja Agency Overview

An Aaja Agency acts as a bridge between the Aaja platform and new hosts. Agencies find, recruit, and support hosts as they join the platform, helping them complete Aaja Host Registration, understand daily and weekly tasks, and grow through the host level system. In exchange, the agency earns a share of its team's combined weekly diamond earnings.

Unlike a typical job, running an Aaja Agency is closer to running a small, remote team. You are not paid a fixed salary — your income is directly tied to how well your hosts perform. This makes agency work most rewarding for people who enjoy recruiting, coaching, and staying consistently engaged with their team.

Growth

Sub-Agency Opportunities

Expand your network and unlock additional rewards by developing sub-agents.

As your Aaja Agency grows, you may be able to recruit and develop sub-agents — smaller agency units that operate under your guidance. Sub-agents recruit and manage their own hosts, while your agency benefits from the combined activity of the wider network. This structure rewards agencies that focus on long-term team building rather than short-term recruitment alone.

Building a sub-agency network takes time and consistent support, but it's one of the most effective ways to scale your Aaja Agency income beyond what a single host team can generate.
