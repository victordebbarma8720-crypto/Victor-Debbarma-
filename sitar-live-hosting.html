Sitar Live Host Registration in India

Become a Sitar Live Host

Sitar Live hosting provides eligible applicants with an opportunity to earn through private video calls, match calls, eligible virtual gifts and interactive entertainment. Hosts may communicate with users, perform talents such as singing or dancing, conduct talk shows and create friendly conversations. Host earnings depend on activity, call traffic, call duration, gifts, host level, user demand and compliance with platform rules. Income is not guaranteed.

Working Standards

Sitar Live Host Working Requirements

Hosts should maintain a professional and comfortable working environment during video calls.

✅ Hosts Should

  • ✓Wear suitable clothing
  • ✓Maintain a presentable appearance
  • ✓Use bright lighting
  • ✓Keep the camera stable
  • ✓Position the camera correctly
  • ✓Use a clean background
  • ✓Greet users when calls connect
  • ✓Communicate politely
  • ✓Keep conversations engaging
  • ✓Respond regularly to calls
  • ✓Follow platform rules

🚫 Hosts Should Avoid

  • ✗Working in a dark room
  • ✗Blocking the camera
  • ✗Using an untidy background
  • ✗Remaining silent during calls
  • ✗Allowing another person to use the account
  • ✗Sharing passwords or login details
  • ✗Creating duplicate accounts
  • ✗Sharing private contact information
  • ✗Violating content policies
