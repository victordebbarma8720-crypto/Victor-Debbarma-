How to Avoid Fake Sitar Live Agency Offers

Spotting Suspicious Recruiters and Protecting Yourself

As Sitar Live opportunities have grown in popularity, so have fake recruiters. Here are the warning signs to watch for.

Common Warning Signs

  • Upfront payment demands — especially via personal UPI IDs or bank accounts before any verification
  • Guaranteed income claims — real income is performance-based, never guaranteed
  • Requests for sensitive info — passwords, OTPs, PINs or recovery codes are never needed
  • Pressure and urgency — "slots closing today" tactics aren't how real registration works
  • Unofficial app sources — see our safe app-download guide

How to Verify a Recruiter

  • Confirm details with an established support channel before sharing documents
  • Never pay before verification is complete
  • Be wary of anyone avoiding written communication
