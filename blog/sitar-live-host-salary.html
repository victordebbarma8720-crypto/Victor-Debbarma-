Sitar Live Host Salary and Earnings

How Host Income Is Actually Calculated

"What is the Sitar Live host salary?" There isn't a fixed one — income is performance-based. Here's how earnings actually work.

No Guaranteed Fixed Salary

Hosts earn coins through private video calls, match calls and eligible virtual gifts, later converted to real currency. Two hosts working the same hours can earn very differently depending on activity and performance.

Private Video Calls

The stated rate ranges from 600 to 2,800 coins per minute, depending on account level — from Level 0 (600 coins/min) up to Level 6 (2,800 coins/min).

Match Calls & Gifts

A match call lasts ~30 seconds and pays 100 coins. Eligible virtual gifts from users also convert to coins as part of weekly earnings.

Converting Coins to Money

Roughly 10,000 coins = $1. Minimum withdrawal is 100,000 coins (~$10). See our weekly payment guide for the full conversion table.

What Drives Higher Earnings

  • Answer rate (aim for 60%+) and call duration (70+ seconds)
  • Account level, which raises the per-minute rate
  • User traffic and policy compliance
Income is performance-based and not guaranteed. Anyone promising a fixed salary isn't describing how the platform works.
