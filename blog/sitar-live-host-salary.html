Sitar Live Host Salary and Earnings

How Host Income Is Actually Calculated

One of the most common questions from applicants is "what is the Sitar Live host salary?" The honest answer is that there is no fixed salary — host income is performance-based and depends on several factors working together. Here's how the earning system actually works.

There Is No Guaranteed Fixed Salary

Sitar Live hosting does not pay a guaranteed fixed monthly or weekly salary. Instead, hosts earn coins through private video calls, match calls and eligible virtual gifts, which are later converted to real currency. This means two hosts working the same number of hours can end up with very different earnings depending on activity, traffic and performance.

Private Video Calls

The stated private-call rate ranges from 600 to 2,800 coins per minute, depending on the host's current account level. Level is generally determined by accumulated eligible income — the more a host earns over time, the higher their level and per-minute rate climb, from Level 0 (600 coins/min) up to Level 6 (2,800 coins/min).

Match Calls

A match call lasts approximately 30 seconds and the stated reward is 100 coins per completed eligible match call. These shorter interactions add up over a busy day and are a meaningful part of total weekly income for active hosts.

Virtual Gifts

Users can send eligible virtual gifts during conversations, which convert to coins as part of a host's weekly earnings. Engaging, friendly conversation tends to correlate with more gifts, though this is never guaranteed.

Converting Coins to Money

Coins convert to real currency at a stated rate of roughly 10,000 coins to $1. The minimum withdrawal is 100,000 coins, approximately $10. See our full weekly payment guide for the complete coin-conversion table and withdrawal schedule.

What Actually Drives Higher Earnings

  • Consistent call activity and answer rate (aim for 60%+)
  • Average call duration (aim for 70+ seconds)
  • Account level, which increases the per-minute rate
  • User traffic and demand, which vary day to day
  • Compliance with platform policies
Income is performance-based and not guaranteed. Anyone promising a fixed guaranteed salary for Sitar Live hosting is not describing how the platform actually works.
