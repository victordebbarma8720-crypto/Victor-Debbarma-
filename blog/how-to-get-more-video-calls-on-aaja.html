How to Get More Video Calls on Aaja

Every Aaja host asks the same question sooner or later: how do I get more video calls? It's the single biggest factor in how much time you spend on the platform actually pays off. The good news is that call volume isn't random luck. It responds to a handful of things you can actually control — your profile, your schedule, and how you show up during a call. This guide covers all three, along with the small mistakes that quietly push callers away.

Why Video Calls Matter for Hosts

Video calls are the core activity on Aaja. Everything else — your level, your visibility, your standing with your agency — tends to follow from how many calls you take and how well they go. A host who takes a steady stream of calls each week builds momentum: more calls usually lead to a stronger profile, and a stronger profile tends to attract even more calls. That's why it's worth treating call volume as something to actively manage rather than something that just happens to you.

It's also worth remembering that call volume and call quality reinforce each other. A host who takes ten rushed, distracted calls a day often builds a weaker reputation than one who takes five calls with genuine attention and warmth. Callers remember how a conversation felt far more than how long it lasted, and that feeling is what brings them back and encourages them to recommend you to others.

If you're new to the platform and still getting oriented, it helps to first read our Aaja Hosting overview, which explains how the host role fits together before you focus on optimizing any single part of it.

Optimize Your Profile First

Before anything else, look at your profile the way a stranger would. This is the first — and sometimes only — thing a potential caller sees before deciding whether to connect with you. A few adjustments that consistently help:

  • Use a clear, well-lit photo. Avoid group photos, heavy filters, or dark or blurry images.
  • Write a short, honest bio. A couple of lines about what callers can expect from talking to you goes a long way.
  • Keep your display name simple and readable. Avoid symbols or text that's hard to read at a glance.
  • Fill in every optional field. Incomplete profiles often get skipped over in favor of complete ones.

Think of your profile as a first impression that never sleeps — it works for you even when you're offline. It's also worth revisiting your profile every few weeks rather than setting it once and forgetting about it. A photo that felt current a few months ago might not represent you as well today, and a bio that mentions something specific about your interests can make you feel more approachable than a generic one-line description.

Best Times to Be Online

Timing matters more than most new hosts expect. Being online during hours when more people are actively browsing the app naturally increases your chances of being seen. Evenings and weekends tend to see higher activity for most hosts, though this can vary depending on who your typical callers are. Rather than guessing, pay attention to your own patterns over a couple of weeks — note when you tend to get called the most, and try to be online consistently during those windows.

It also helps to avoid going online for very short, unpredictable bursts. A caller browsing the app is more likely to reach a host who's reliably available than one who logs on for ten minutes and disappears. If your schedule genuinely varies day to day, consider setting a rough weekly pattern instead — for example, mornings on some days and evenings on others — rather than leaving it entirely open-ended. Even an inconsistent-but-tracked schedule beats a completely random one, because it gives you data to work with.

Keep Callers Engaged

Getting a call is only half the job — keeping it going well matters just as much, because a good experience is what brings a caller back and encourages them to recommend you. A few habits that help:

  • Greet callers warmly and use their name if it's shared.
  • Ask open questions instead of ones that end the conversation quickly.
  • Stay relaxed and let conversations breathe — not every call needs to be high energy.
  • Be respectful and patient, especially with first-time callers who may be nervous too.

For more detail on the mechanics of a good call, see our dedicated guide on video calling tips for Aaja hosts. It's also worth paying attention to how you end a call, not just how you start one. A warm, unhurried goodbye leaves a much better impression than an abrupt disconnect, and it's often the last thing a caller remembers before deciding whether to call you again.

Promote Yourself the Right Way

Many successful hosts quietly promote their own profile outside the app, sharing it with friends, followers, or their existing network where platform rules allow it. This isn't about aggressive marketing — it's simply letting people who already know and trust you discover that you're on Aaja. Word-of-mouth referrals often lead to some of the most comfortable, longer-lasting calls, since the caller already has a sense of who you are.

Hosts who treat their profile like a small personal brand — consistent, honest, and easy to recognize — tend to build a steadier stream of repeat callers over time.

If you're unsure what's allowed when it comes to self-promotion, our support team can walk you through it — reach out any time via our contact page. A simple, honest post mentioning that you're now hosting on Aaja, without exaggerated claims about earnings, tends to work better than anything that sounds like a hard sell. People respond to authenticity far more than polished marketing language.

Why Consistency Beats Bursts

It's tempting to go all-in for a few days and then take a long break, especially when results feel slow at first. But Aaja, like most call and streaming platforms, tends to reward hosts who show up reliably. A predictable schedule — even a modest one — usually outperforms sporadic, high-effort sprints followed by silence. If you can only commit an hour a day, doing that consistently for a month will likely serve you better than four intense days followed by two weeks of inactivity.

This is also one of the areas where an Aaja Agency can help — a good agency notices when a host's activity dips and can offer a nudge or support before momentum is lost entirely. If you're hosting independently without an agency, consider setting your own simple check-in, such as reviewing your activity every Sunday evening, so a quiet week doesn't turn into a quiet month without you noticing.

Common Mistakes That Reduce Calls

A few habits quietly work against hosts trying to grow their call volume:

  1. Leaving the profile half-finished after registration.
  2. Going online at random, unpredictable hours.
  3. Ending calls abruptly or seeming distracted.
  4. Ignoring feedback from an agency or from repeat callers.
  5. Comparing your numbers to other hosts instead of tracking your own progress over time.

Many of these overlap with broader beginner pitfalls — our guide on common mistakes new Aaja hosts make covers several more in depth. If you'd like personal feedback on your profile or schedule, our team is happy to take a look — just message us on WhatsApp.
