How to Become a Kamaichat Host

A step-by-step walkthrough of the Kamaichat host registration journey, from first contact to your first qualifying call.

If you are searching for how to become a Kamaichat host, this guide walks through the registration journey end to end, based on the information and host policy provided to us. Kamaichat is a video chat platform where eligible hosts can participate in video calls, messaging and qualifying tasks.

Step 1: Check Basic Eligibility

Before starting Kamaichat host registration, confirm you meet the baseline requirements: at least 18 years of age, comfortable communicating with users on video calls, a compatible Android or iPhone smartphone, and a stable internet connection. See our full Kamaichat host requirements page for the complete checklist.

Step 2: Contact Our Team on WhatsApp

Registration starts with a conversation. Message our team on WhatsApp and let us know you want to apply as a Kamaichat host. We will walk you through the current registration steps and answer any questions about the process.

Step 3: Submit Your Profile Information

You will be asked to share the information and profile materials requested for registration, including a suitable profile and cover photo. A clear, genuine profile photo is typically part of the review process.

Step 4: Complete the Review

Your submitted information may be reviewed according to the applicable platform requirements. Review timelines can vary, so it is worth confirming current expectations with our team when you apply.

Step 5: Get Approved and Start Participating

Once approved, you can log in and begin participating in qualifying video calls, messages and daily tasks. Many new hosts start by reviewing the Kamaichat host task rewards breakdown so they understand which activities count toward rewards from day one.

What Happens After Registration?

After your Kamaichat host registration is approved, rewards are tracked in coins, which convert to INR at the rate described in our coin to INR conversion guide. Payments follow the weekly UPI schedule explained in our payment guide.

Actual approval, rewards and payment outcomes depend on eligibility, activity and the platform's current policies, which may change. This guide reflects the information provided to us and is not a guarantee of earnings or approval.
