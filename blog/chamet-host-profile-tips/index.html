Preparing a Professional Chamet Host Profile

Your profile is the first thing users see. These practical tips help you present yourself clearly and professionally while staying within the platform’s rules.

In this article

  1. Use a clear, recent profile photo
  2. Get the lighting right
  3. Choose an appropriate background
  4. Write a short, honest description
  5. Communicate respectfully
  6. Protect your privacy and account
  7. Profile checklist

Use a clear, recent profile photo

Choose a recent photo that clearly shows your face. Avoid heavy filters that make you hard to recognise, and make sure the image follows the app’s content rules. If identity or face verification is required, a photo that matches your appearance helps avoid confusion.

Get the lighting right

  • Face a window or a soft light source rather than having light behind you.
  • Avoid harsh overhead lighting that casts shadows.
  • A simple ring light or desk lamp can help for evening sessions.

Choose an appropriate background

A tidy, neutral background looks professional and keeps attention on you. Remove anything personal you would not want strangers to see, such as documents, addresses or photos of family members.

Write a short, honest description

Keep your profile description friendly and accurate. Mention languages you speak and topics you enjoy talking about. Do not include phone numbers, social media handles or other contact details unless the platform rules explicitly allow it.

Communicate respectfully

Polite, respectful communication is part of a professional profile. Follow the platform’s conduct guidelines, and end or report conversations that make you uncomfortable or break the rules.

Protect your privacy and account

  • Do not share your home address, workplace or financial details.
  • Never share your password or one-time verification codes — including with an agency.
  • Use a strong, unique password and keep your phone’s software up to date.

Profile checklist

  • Clear, recent photo that follows the content rules.
  • Good lighting and a neutral background.
  • Short, accurate description.
  • No personal contact or location details.
  • Account secured with a strong password.
