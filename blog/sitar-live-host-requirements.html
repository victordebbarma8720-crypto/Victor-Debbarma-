Sitar Live Host Requirements

Age, Profile, Device and Verification Requirements Explained

Before applying for Sitar Live hosting, it helps to know exactly what's expected of applicants — not just to increase your chances of approval, but to understand whether the role fits your situation. Here's a full breakdown of the requirements.

Age and Gender Eligibility

Sitar Live hosting is generally open to female and transgender applicants between 18 and 35 years old, subject to current eligibility, verification and profile requirements. These requirements may be updated by Sitar Live at any time, so it's worth confirming current eligibility with our support team before applying.

Device and Connectivity

Hosts need a smartphone or device with a good front camera capable of clear video calls, along with a stable internet connection. A quiet, well-lit space with a clean background is also important — call quality and appearance directly affect how consistently a host receives calls.

Profile and Appearance Standards

  • Comfortable appearing on video calls
  • Friendly and confident on camera
  • Able to maintain a presentable appearance
  • Good communication skills
  • Able to keep conversations engaging

Verification Requirements

All applicants must be able to complete identity and profile verification, and must provide genuine information throughout the process. Talents such as singing, dancing, storytelling or entertainment skills can be helpful but are not compulsory — communication ability and consistency matter more for most hosts.

What Isn't Required

Previous experience with live-streaming or video-calling apps may be beneficial but isn't a strict requirement. Many successful hosts start with no prior platform experience and build up their performance over time.

Meeting these requirements does not guarantee approval — final eligibility and account decisions are controlled by Sitar Live. See our Sitar Live Hosting page for full earnings and level details.
