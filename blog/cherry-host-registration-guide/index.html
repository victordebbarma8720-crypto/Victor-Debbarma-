Cherry Host Registration: Requirements and Process

Cherry host registration explained: who can apply, what you need, the step-by-step process, and how to prepare for your first week of Cherry hosting.

By CherryOfficial.in Updated 27 October 2026 6 min read

In this article

  1. Host requirements
  2. Your hosting setup
  3. The registration process
  4. Your first week
  5. Staying safe as a host
  6. FAQs

Cherry host registration is the first step to taking calls on the Cherry app. This guide explains who can apply, what you need and how the process works. For a summary, see the Cherry host registration page.

Host requirements

Confirm the current requirements with us before you start. Commonly requested requirements are:

  • You meet the Cherry app’s minimum age requirement (adults only).
  • You can complete the app’s identity or profile verification.
  • You agree to follow Cherry’s terms and community guidelines.
  • You can commit to a regular schedule, including evening rush hours in IST.

Your hosting setup

  • A smartphone with the official Cherry app
  • A stable internet connection
  • A quiet, tidy and well-lit space
  • Headphones for clear audio

The registration process

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 8132958338 and say you want to become a Cherry host.
  2. Install the official Cherry app. Read our Cherry app guide to avoid fake downloads.
  3. Create your profile using accurate information and an appropriate photo.
  4. Complete verification as required by the app.
  5. Receive your schedule once approved. Approval is not guaranteed.

Your first week

The weekly base salary plan supplied to us expects 3–5 hours online during the 9 PM–2 AM IST rush hours, an answer rate above 90%, an average call duration above 80 seconds, and attendance according to your schedule. Use your first week to build habits:

  • Only go online when you are ready to answer calls.
  • Check your answer rate and average call duration daily.
  • Ask your agency for feedback early.

Full details: Cherry weekly base salary plan: rules and eligibility.

Staying safe as a host

  • Never share your password, login codes or payment details with anyone, including agencies.
  • Do not share personal contact details with users.
  • Report abusive users using the app’s reporting tools.
  • Be cautious of anyone asking for money to register you or guarantee income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cherry host registration free?

We have not been given any official host registration fee to publish. Ask us for current terms, and be cautious of anyone asking for money to guarantee approval.

Can I host from anywhere?

Hosting is done through the app, but the supplied salary plan uses India Standard Time for rush hours, so plan your schedule around 9 PM–2 AM IST.

How soon can I start?

After registration and verification are complete and approved. Timelines vary and are not guaranteed.
