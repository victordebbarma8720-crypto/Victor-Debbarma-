Cherry Agency questions

What is a Cherry agency?

A partner that recruits, onboards and supports hosts on the Cherry app. See Cherry Agency registration.

How do I register an agency?

Message us on WhatsApp, confirm the current requirements, install the official app and complete the application. Full steps: agency registration guide.

Is agency approval guaranteed?

No. Applications are reviewed through the official Cherry process.

Cherry hosting questions

What does a Cherry host do?

Hosts take calls with users on the Cherry app during their assigned schedule, following community guidelines.

What do I need to become a host?

You must meet the app’s age and verification requirements and have a smartphone, the official app and a stable connection. See the host registration guide.

Salary plan questions

What is the Cherry weekly base salary?

The plan supplied to us advertises $21–$35 per week for hosts who meet all of its conditions. It is not guaranteed.

What are the conditions?

3–5 hours online during 9 PM–2 AM IST, an answer rate above 90%, an average call duration above 80 seconds and attendance according to your assigned schedule. Read the salary plan explainer.

Cherry app questions

Where do I get the Cherry app?

Only from an official app store listing or official Cherry source. See the Cherry app guide.
