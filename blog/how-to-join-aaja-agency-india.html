How to Join Aaja Agency in India

If you've been searching for how to join Aaja as an agency partner, you're likely looking for a business you can run from home, on your own schedule, with earning potential tied directly to your effort. Starting an Aaja Agency is exactly that — a role focused on recruiting, training, and supporting hosts on the Aaja video call platform. This guide walks through everything you need to know before you register.

What Is an Aaja Agency?

An Aaja Agency is a partner role within the Aaja ecosystem. Instead of hosting video calls yourself, you recruit and manage a team of hosts. Your agency helps new hosts complete registration, understand daily and weekly tasks, and grow through the host level system. In return, your agency earns commission based on your team's combined weekly performance.

Think of it less like a traditional job and more like running a small remote team. There's no fixed salary — your income scales with how active and successful your hosts are, which is why recruitment quality and ongoing support matter so much.

Who Can Join as an Agency?

You don't need previous experience in the live streaming or video call industry to become an Aaja Agency. What matters more is:

  • Being 18 years or older
  • Having some ability to reach and recruit potential hosts, whether through social media, personal networks, or word of mouth
  • Good communication skills to guide and support your team
  • A stable internet connection and a bank account or UPI ID for receiving settlements
  • Time and consistency — agencies that check in with their hosts regularly tend to perform better

If this sounds like you, the next step is understanding exactly how registration works.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

Joining Aaja as an agency follows a simple process:

  • Step 1 — Reach out: Message our support team on WhatsApp and let them know you're interested in Aaja Agency Registration.
  • Step 2 — Share your details: Provide your name, contact number, and preferred payment method for future settlements.
  • Step 3 — Get onboarded: Our team walks you through agency setup, explains the commission structure, and shares practical recruitment tips.
  • Step 4 — Start recruiting: Begin reaching out to potential hosts and guide them through Aaja Host Registration.
  • Step 5 — Support your team: Stay in touch with your hosts, help them understand daily tasks, and encourage consistent activity.

For the full breakdown of eligibility and benefits, see our complete Aaja Agency Registration page.

What You'll Be Responsible For

Once registered, an Aaja Agency is generally responsible for:

  • Recruiting hosts who meet basic eligibility and can commit to regular activity.
  • Guiding onboarding so new hosts understand how to complete diamond earning and task systems.
  • Maintaining quality by ensuring hosts follow Aaja's community guidelines.
  • Growing the network over time, potentially developing sub-agents as your team expands.

How Commission Works

Agency commission is calculated as a share of your team's total weekly diamond earnings. As your team becomes larger and more consistently active, your agency can move into higher commission tiers. Because tiers and exact percentages can be updated by the Aaja platform, we recommend confirming current rates directly with our support team — see our full Aaja Agency Commission Structure guide for a deeper breakdown.

The most successful agencies focus less on one-time recruitment and more on ongoing host support — a small, consistently active team often outperforms a large, inactive one.

Tips for New Agencies

A few practical habits that help new agencies get off to a strong start:

  • Set clear expectations with new hosts from day one, including how tasks and diamonds work.
  • Check in weekly with your team to spot issues early and keep motivation high.
  • Share simple host tips with your recruits so they ramp up faster.
  • Be patient — agency income builds gradually as your network matures.
