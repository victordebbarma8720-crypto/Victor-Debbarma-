Kamaichat Payment Guide

How and when Kamaichat host payments are made, according to the information provided to us.

Understanding the Kamaichat payment schedule helps you plan around when to expect your payout. Here is what we have been told about host payments.

Payment Method: UPI

Payments are made via UPI, so having valid and up-to-date payment details on file is important during registration.

Currency: INR

All payments are made in Indian Rupees, converted from your accumulated coins using the rate in our coin to INR conversion guide.

Payment Frequency and Schedule

According to the provided information, payments are scheduled weekly, on Tuesday, before 23:00 GMT+5:30.

Minimum Cash-Out

The stated minimum cash-out requirement is 30,000 coins / ₹291. Coins below this amount typically roll over until the threshold is reached.

What to Do If a Payment Looks Delayed

Payment timing and eligibility may depend on verification, platform requirements and applicable policies. If a scheduled payment seems delayed, contact our team on WhatsApp so we can help you check your status.

Payment timing, methods and eligibility may change. Always verify the latest applicable information before relying on a specific payout date.
