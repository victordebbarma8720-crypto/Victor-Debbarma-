Understanding Chamet Agency Commission: How the Calculation Works

Agency commission is one of the most searched — and most misunderstood — topics around Chamet agency registration. This guide explains the general calculation and why every figure must be checked against current terms.

In this article

  1. The basic formula
  2. Illustrative examples
  3. Direct hosts and sub-agencies
  4. About historical commission tables
  5. Questions to ask before relying on a rate
  6. Key points

The basic formula

Most revenue-share arrangements can be described with one simple formula:

Eligible commission = Eligible commissionable revenue × Applicable commission rate

Each part of that formula needs to be defined by the current program terms:

  • Eligible commissionable revenue — which activity counts, over what period, and after which deductions.
  • Applicable commission rate — the percentage that applies, which may depend on a level, tier or other condition.
  • Eligibility — whether the agency and its hosts meet the conditions for commission to be paid at all.

Illustrative examples

The numbers below are hypothetical. They show how the arithmetic works and are not current Chamet rates or promised payments.

Notice that the formula is simple, but the inputs are not. A different definition of “eligible revenue” or a different rate changes the result completely.

Direct hosts and sub-agencies

Some agency programs distinguish between commission earned from hosts an agency recruits directly and commission related to sub-agencies. How — or whether — sub-agency commission is calculated depends entirely on the authorised program. Do not assume it follows the same formula, uses the same rate or is available at all.

About historical commission tables

Tables listing agency “classes” with revenue thresholds and percentages circulate online. Such tables are often old, and it is frequently unclear whether the thresholds are monthly figures, cumulative totals or something else. We do not treat any such table as current policy. The agency page shows one historical table, clearly labelled, purely for context.

Questions to ask before relying on a rate

  • Where is the rate published, and is that source authorised?
  • When was it last updated?
  • What exactly counts as eligible revenue, and over which period?
  • Are there deductions, adjustments or minimum thresholds?
  • When and how is commission paid out?

Key points

  • The formula is: eligible revenue × applicable rate.
  • All examples on this website are illustrative only.
  • Commission is not guaranteed and rates can change.
  • Confirm current terms through authorised channels.
