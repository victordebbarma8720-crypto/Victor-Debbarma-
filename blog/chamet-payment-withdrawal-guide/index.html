Chamet Payment and Withdrawal Guide: What to Verify

Payment rules decide when an in-app balance actually becomes money in your account. This guide lists what to verify and explains why older figures should not be relied on.

In this article

  1. Why payment rules matter
  2. What to verify
  3. Historical information we have not verified
  4. Protect your payments
  5. If something goes wrong

Why payment rules matter

For hosts and agencies alike, the practical value of any balance or commission depends on the payment and withdrawal rules. Two people with the same in-app balance can have very different outcomes if one has not completed verification or does not meet a minimum withdrawal amount.

What to verify

Payment and withdrawal checklist
ItemQuestion to answer
Minimum withdrawalWhat is the smallest amount that can be withdrawn?
IncrementsMust withdrawals be made in fixed multiples?
VerificationIs identity or face verification required before withdrawing?
Payment methodsWhich methods are supported in your country?
Wallet linkingDo you need to link a wallet or account, and how?
Processing timeHow long do withdrawals usually take?
Fees & adjustmentsAre there fees, deductions or currency adjustments?
EligibilityWhat account conditions must be met to withdraw?

Historical information we have not verified

Older material supplied to us mentions a $10 minimum withdrawal, withdrawals in multiples of $10, balance carryover for amounts below the minimum, face verification and a wallet referred to as Metwallet. We have not been able to confirm that any of these are current requirements. Treat them as historical notes only and check the current rules inside the official app or through authorised documentation.

Protect your payments

  • Never share your password, one-time codes or wallet credentials.
  • Link only payment accounts in your own name.
  • Be cautious of anyone offering to “speed up” a withdrawal for a fee.
  • Keep records of withdrawals and any communication about them.

If something goes wrong

If a withdrawal is delayed or rejected, check the in-app messages and help sections first, and contact the platform’s official support channel. An agency can help explain the process but cannot override platform decisions.

For background on in-app balances, read Understanding Chamet Beans conversion and the payment section of the hosting guide.
