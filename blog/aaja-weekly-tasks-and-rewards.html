Aaja Weekly Tasks and Rewards Explained

While daily tasks reward hosts for showing up each day, weekly tasks and rewards look at the bigger picture — your total activity and performance across a full seven-day cycle. Understanding how weekly rewards work can help you plan your time more effectively and avoid last-minute scrambling to hit a goal before the week resets.

What Are Aaja Weekly Tasks?

Weekly tasks are broader activity goals that track your cumulative performance over the week rather than resetting daily. They're generally built around diamond milestones, total call time, or a combination of both, and they reward hosts who maintain strong performance across the entire week rather than just a single strong day.

This system is designed to reward consistency over the course of a week, complementing the shorter-term structure of daily tasks.

For many hosts, weekly rewards end up being the more meaningful bonus compared to daily ones, simply because they reflect sustained effort rather than a single day's activity. Understanding how the weekly cycle is structured — when it starts, when it resets, and what's being measured — helps you plan your week more intentionally instead of reacting to task notifications as they appear.

Weekly Tasks vs. Daily Tasks

It helps to think of daily and weekly tasks as two layers of the same reward system:

  • Daily tasks reset every 24 hours and reward short, repeatable actions like logging in or hitting a minimum call time for that day.
  • Weekly tasks accumulate across the full week and reward hosts who maintain that activity consistently, often unlocking larger bonus diamonds than any single daily task.

The two systems work together — strong daily habits are usually what get you to your weekly milestones in the first place.

Some hosts find it useful to picture daily tasks as the individual bricks and weekly milestones as the wall they build. Miss a brick here or there and the wall still stands, but consistently skipping days will eventually show up as a much smaller weekly total than a host who shows up reliably, even for shorter sessions, throughout the week.

Weekly Diamond Milestones

Many hosts encounter weekly diamond milestones, where reaching a certain cumulative diamond total during the week unlocks a bonus reward. These milestones are one of the reasons steady, everyday effort tends to outperform occasional long sessions — hitting a milestone requires activity spread across the week, not crammed into one day.

Spreading your call time evenly across the week, rather than trying to make up for lost days all at once, is generally the more reliable path to hitting weekly milestones.

Exact milestone thresholds can be adjusted by the Aaja platform, so always check the current goals directly in the app or confirm with our support team if anything looks unclear.

It's also useful to understand that weekly milestones typically layer on top of your regular call and gift earnings rather than replacing them. In other words, you're not choosing between earning through calls or earning through milestones — both contribute to your overall weekly total, with the milestone bonus acting as an extra reward for hitting a cumulative threshold.

Planning Your Week Around Tasks

A little planning goes a long way with weekly tasks. Consider mapping out your week like this:

  1. Check your current weekly goals at the start of the cycle so you know what you're working toward.
  2. Break the total requirement down into daily chunks rather than leaving it for the last day or two.
  3. Build in a buffer day in case you have to skip a session due to other commitments.
  4. Review your progress midweek so you can adjust if you're falling behind.

This kind of light planning is especially useful for hosts balancing Aaja hosting with a job, studies, or family responsibilities.

A simple weekly planning habit that many hosts find helpful is jotting down, even informally, roughly how many hours they intend to host each day of the week. This doesn't need to be rigid — plans change — but having a rough outline makes it much easier to notice early in the week if you're falling behind, rather than realizing on the final day that you're short of your goal with no time left to catch up.

How Agencies Support Weekly Performance

If you're part of an Aaja Agency, your agency has a direct incentive to help you stay on track with weekly goals, since your team's combined weekly performance affects their commission too. A good agency will often check in midweek, remind hosts of upcoming milestones, and help troubleshoot if someone's activity has dropped off. If you're not getting this kind of support, it's worth raising with your agency contact.

Recovering From a Slow Start to the Week

Not every week goes to plan. Illness, unexpected commitments, or simply a busy few days can leave a host behind on their weekly goals partway through the cycle. If this happens, resist the urge to try cramming an entire week's worth of activity into the last day or two — this usually leads to rushed, low-quality calls that hurt engagement rather than help it.

Instead, recalculate what's realistically achievable in the time remaining and adjust your target accordingly. It's better to make solid, genuine progress toward a slightly reduced goal than to burn out chasing the original target through low-quality effort. Missing a single weekly milestone occasionally is normal and not something to be discouraged by — what matters more is your overall trend across several weeks.

Tips for Hitting Weekly Goals Consistently

A few habits that help hosts consistently hit their weekly targets:

  • Treat weekly goals as a running total, checking in every couple of days rather than only at the end.
  • Pair weekly planning with your daily task routine so both systems reinforce each other.
  • Avoid burnout by spreading effort evenly instead of doing marathon sessions right before the week resets.
  • Read our beginner tips for more ways to stay consistent as a new host.

It also helps to separate the idea of "hitting a goal" from "doing your best work." Some of the most reliable hosts aren't necessarily the ones who technically complete every single weekly task — they're the ones who show up consistently, give genuine effort in each call, and let the milestones follow naturally from that. Chasing numbers directly can sometimes lead to shortcuts that hurt call quality, while focusing on consistent, genuine engagement tends to produce strong weekly numbers as a side effect rather than the primary goal.
