How to Download the Sitar Live App Safely

Avoiding Fake and Modified APK Files

Once your Sitar Live registration is approved, installing the app correctly matters — fake or modified app files circulate online and can put your account, personal information and device at risk. Here's how to install the authorised app safely.

Only Install After Approval

The authorised Sitar Live app is generally provided or linked by your registration support team after your host or agency application is approved. If someone offers you the app before you've registered or completed verification, treat that as a warning sign rather than a shortcut.

Signs of a Fake or Modified APK

  • The file is shared through a random link, forum or unfamiliar file-sharing site instead of an official channel
  • The app asks for unusual permissions unrelated to video calling
  • The installer promises "unlocked" features, bonus coins or guaranteed earnings for installing a modified version
  • The source cannot confirm it is connected to your approved registration

Safe Installation Steps

  1. Complete registration and verification first.
  2. Get the official download link or instructions from your registration support contact.
  3. Avoid downloading APK files shared in random group chats.
  4. Do not enter your login details into any app you were not directed to by verified support.
  5. Contact support immediately if you're unsure about a download source.

Protecting Your Account After Installation

Never share your password, OTP, or login details with anyone — not even someone claiming to be from your agency or Sitar Live support. Legitimate support will never ask you to hand over your credentials.

Use only authorised registration and application-download sources. If you're ever unsure, contact our support team on WhatsApp before installing anything.
