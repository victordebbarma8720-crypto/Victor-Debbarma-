Cherry Weekly Base Salary Plan: Rules and Eligibility

A clear explanation of the Cherry weekly base salary plan supplied to us: the advertised $21–$35 range, rush hours, answer rate, call duration and attendance rules, and what to verify before relying on it.

The Cherry weekly base salary is one of the most-asked-about topics among new hosts. This guide explains the weekly base salary plan that was supplied to us, what each condition means in practice, and what you should confirm before you count on it. If you are new to hosting, start with our Cherry host registration page.

What is the Cherry weekly base salary?

A weekly base salary is a fixed amount offered to hosts who meet a set of conditions during a week. It is separate from anything else a host might earn on the platform. In the plan supplied to us, the advertised weekly base salary is $21–$35 per week.

The key word is conditions. The base salary is linked to how long you are online during busy hours, how many calls you answer, how long your calls last on average, and whether you keep to your schedule. If the conditions are not met, the base salary may not apply.

Rush hours and time online

The supplied plan uses India Standard Time and defines the rush-hour window as 9 PM to 2 AM IST. Hosts are expected to be online for 3–5 hours within that window.

Rush hours are when the most users are expected to be active, so being online then matters more than being online at quiet times. If you live outside India, convert the window to your own local time and plan around it.

  • Block out your rush-hour session in advance, as you would a work shift.
  • Charge your phone and check your connection before 9 PM IST.
  • If you can only do the minimum, aim slightly above 3 hours so a dropped connection does not put you short.

Call answer rate above 90%

Your answer rate is the share of incoming calls you pick up while you are online. The supplied plan requires it to be above 90%, which means answering more than 9 out of every 10 calls.

Missed calls add up quickly. Ten missed calls out of 80 already takes you to 87.5%, below the target. The simplest way to protect your answer rate is to only go online when you are actually ready to take calls, and to go offline when you need a break.

Average call duration above 80 seconds

The plan asks for an average call duration above 80 seconds. This is an average across your calls, so a few short calls will not ruin it, but a pattern of very short calls will.

  • Greet callers warmly and by name if it is shown.
  • Ask open questions that invite a conversation rather than a yes or no answer.
  • Make sure your camera, lighting and audio are good so users do not hang up early.
  • Always stay within Cherry’s community guidelines; longer calls never justify breaking the rules.

Attendance and your schedule

The plan requires attendance according to the assigned schedule. Your agency normally shares this schedule with you. Being consistently present on your assigned days and hours is part of qualifying, not just the total number of hours.

If you cannot make a session, tell your agency as early as possible and ask how it affects your eligibility for that week.

Who is eligible?

Based on the supplied plan, a host would need to:

  1. Be a registered, verified Cherry host who meets the app’s age and account requirements.
  2. Be online 3–5 hours during the 9 PM–2 AM IST rush-hour window.
  3. Keep a call answer rate above 90%.
  4. Keep an average call duration above 80 seconds.
  5. Attend according to their assigned schedule.

There may be other conditions we have not been told about, such as minimum weeks of activity or account-standing requirements. Ask your agency for the complete, current list in writing. Our Cherry host registration guide covers the first step: getting registered.

Why the salary is not guaranteed

The $21–$35 figure is the range advertised in the plan supplied to us. It is not a guaranteed payment, for several reasons:

  • It only applies if every condition is met for the week.
  • The official rules, amounts and conditions can change at any time.
  • Approval as a host, and continued eligibility, are decided through the official Cherry process.
  • Account issues, policy violations or verification problems can affect eligibility.

Be wary of anyone who promises a fixed weekly income regardless of performance, or who asks you for money to “unlock” a salary.

Practical tips to meet the conditions

  • Plan your week: write down your assigned schedule and your rush-hour sessions.
  • Prepare your space: a quiet, well-lit spot with a stable connection reduces dropped and short calls.
  • Track your numbers: check your answer rate and average call duration regularly, not just at the end of the week.
  • Take breaks properly: go offline for breaks instead of leaving calls unanswered.
  • Talk to your agency: ask for feedback early if your numbers are slipping.

What to verify before you rely on the plan

Before you plan around the Cherry weekly base salary, confirm these points with your agency or official Cherry channels:

  1. Is this plan still active, and does it apply to you?
  2. How is your amount within the $21–$35 range decided?
  3. Are there any extra conditions not listed above?
  4. How and when are payments made, and in what currency?
  5. What happens if you miss one condition in a week?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Cherry weekly base salary?

The plan supplied to us advertises a weekly base salary of $21–$35. It is not guaranteed and depends on meeting all conditions.

What are the Cherry rush hours?

9 PM to 2 AM India Standard Time (IST), according to the supplied plan.

How many hours do I need to be online?

The supplied plan asks for 3–5 hours during the 9 PM–2 AM IST rush-hour window, plus attendance according to your assigned schedule.

What happens if my answer rate falls below 90%?

Under the supplied plan, an answer rate of 90% or below would not meet the condition, which may affect your base salary for that week. Confirm the exact consequences with your agency.

Can the plan change?

Yes. Amounts, conditions and eligibility can change at any time. Always verify the current plan before relying on it.
