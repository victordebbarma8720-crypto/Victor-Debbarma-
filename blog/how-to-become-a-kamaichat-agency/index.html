How to Become a Kamaichat Agency

What it takes to register as a Kamaichat agency and start building a host network.

A Kamaichat agency recruits eligible hosts, assists with registration and onboarding, and provides ongoing support to its host network. Here is how the process typically works, based on the information provided to us.

Step 1: Contact Our Team

Reach out on WhatsApp to express interest in Kamaichat agency registration. We will explain the current process and what is expected of an agency.

Step 2: Provide Required Information

You will be asked to submit the details requested for agency registration so your application can be reviewed.

Step 3: Complete Verification

Your submitted information may be reviewed according to applicable platform requirements before approval as an agency.

Step 4: Receive Approval

Once approved, you can begin operating as a Kamaichat agency and recruiting eligible hosts.

Step 5: Recruit and Support Hosts

Agencies typically recruit eligible hosts, help them complete Kamaichat host registration, explain platform requirements, and support active hosts in their network. See our full agency registration guide for commission details and responsibilities.

Actual commission percentage, calculation method, eligibility requirements and payment conditions depend on the applicable agency arrangement. Agency earnings are not guaranteed.
