Aaja Virtual Gifts Guide: How Gifting Works

📅 Published: April 19, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✍️ Aaja Agency India Team

Virtual gifts are one of the more visible parts of hosting on Aaja — the small animated icons users can send during a call to show appreciation. For new hosts, it's natural to wonder exactly how gifting works, how it connects to actual earnings, and how to encourage it without coming across as pushy. This guide covers all of that honestly.

What Are Virtual Gifts?

Virtual gifts are digital items — often designed as animations or icons — that a user can send a host during a live video call. They're a way for users to express appreciation in the moment, similar to how tipping works in other live-streaming or video call platforms. Each gift generally carries a value that contributes toward the host's overall diamond balance.

If you're still getting familiar with the basics of hosting, our Aaja Hosting page and beginner guide are good starting points before diving deeper into gifting specifics.

It helps to think of gifting the same way you'd think of a tip in any face-to-face service interaction — it's a voluntary gesture from someone who felt the interaction was worth acknowledging. That framing matters because it shapes how you should approach calls: focus on creating a good experience first, and treat any gift you receive as a natural result of that, not a target you're owed.

How Gifting Generally Works

During a match call, users typically have access to a gift menu within the app where they can select and send a gift to the host they're speaking with. This usually happens in real time during the conversation, and hosts often see some kind of visual or audio cue when a gift is sent. Gifting is entirely at the user's discretion — it's not something hosts can trigger or force, which is worth keeping in mind as you build your approach to calls.

Different gifts may have different visual designs and, generally, different associated values, similar to how tipping menus work on other platforms. As a host, you don't need to memorise every gift type in detail — what matters more is understanding that gifting exists as a feature and knowing how to respond graciously when it happens, such as a simple thank you or acknowledgment during the call.

Gifts and Diamonds

Gifts received during calls generally convert into diamonds, which form the core of a host's in-app earnings. This is the same diamond system that's connected to daily and weekly tasks and overall host performance. If diamonds are still a new concept to you, our dedicated Aaja Diamonds Explained guide walks through how the system works in more depth, including how diamonds relate to the withdrawal process.

We intentionally avoid quoting specific exchange rates or gift values here, since these can be set and adjusted by the Aaja platform itself. For current, accurate figures, always confirm directly with our support team rather than relying on unofficial numbers you might see elsewhere.

If you come across figures shared informally online or by people outside official channels, treat them with caution. Values can change, and outdated or inaccurate numbers circulating outside official sources can set the wrong expectations. Confirming with support directly is always the more reliable option.

Encouraging Genuine Gifting

The hosts who receive gifts most consistently over time are usually the ones focused on genuine, enjoyable conversation rather than gifting itself. A few things that tend to help naturally:

  • Being present and attentive rather than distracted during calls.
  • Showing real interest in what the caller is saying, not just waiting for your turn to speak.
  • Maintaining a warm, positive tone even during quieter or less eventful calls.
  • Being consistent — regular callers who feel a genuine connection are more likely to show appreciation over time.
Gifts tend to follow genuine connection, not the other way around. Hosts who focus purely on chasing gifts often come across as less engaging than those who focus on the conversation itself.

What Not to Do

It's important to avoid crossing into behaviour that feels manipulative or pressuring. Directly and repeatedly asking callers for gifts, guilt-tripping them, or making a caller feel obligated tends to backfire — most people respond negatively to pressure, and it can also affect your standing on the platform if it violates community guidelines. Treat every call as a genuine conversation first; any gifting that follows is a natural result of that, not something to chase aggressively.

It's also worth avoiding comparisons with other hosts when it comes to gifting. Every host's audience, schedule, and conversational style are different, and gifting activity naturally varies a lot from person to person and week to week. Measuring your own progress against someone else's highlight reel — which is often what gets shared or talked about — can create unrealistic expectations that don't reflect the normal, gradual reality of building an audience.

Converting Gifts to Earnings

Once gifts have added to your diamond balance, they typically become part of your overall earnings alongside call activity and task completion. From there, the process generally follows the platform's standard withdrawal steps. Our withdrawal process guide explains what that process looks like in general terms, and our payment guide covers related details. As always, reach out through our contact page or WhatsApp if you have specific questions about your own account.

It's worth building the habit of tracking your overall progress loosely over time — not obsessing over every single gift, but noticing broader trends across weeks. Are your diamond totals trending upward as you get more comfortable on calls? Are certain days or approaches to conversation associated with more engagement? This kind of loose, ongoing awareness tends to be more useful than fixating on any one call or any one gift, and it helps you understand what's actually working for you personally.

If you're new to hosting and gifting feels slow to start, that's completely normal. Building the kind of rapport that leads to genuine appreciation from callers takes repetition and practice, just like any conversational skill. Give yourself time to settle into a natural hosting style before judging your gifting activity against any expectation.
