Kamaichat App Download Guide

What you need to know about getting the Kamaichat app set up on your phone before registration.

The Kamaichat app runs on both Android and iPhone devices. This guide covers the basics of getting set up before you begin host or agency registration.

Device Requirements

  • A compatible Android or iPhone smartphone
  • A stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
  • Enough free storage for the app and its updates

Where to Download

We recommend downloading the Kamaichat app from an official app store for your device. If you are unsure which listing is correct, our team can point you in the right direction when you message us on WhatsApp.

After Installing the App

Once the app is installed, the next step for most new users is completing Kamaichat host registration or, if you want to recruit hosts instead, Kamaichat agency registration. Registration and approval typically happen before you can log in and start participating.

Having Trouble With the App?

If you run into issues downloading, installing or logging in, contact our team on WhatsApp. We provide registration and onboarding assistance, including general app-related guidance.

App availability, download sources and requirements are set by the platform and may change. We are not the official Kamaichat app or company — see our disclaimer for details.
