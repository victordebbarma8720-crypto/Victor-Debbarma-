Sitar Live Agency Kaise Le

Sitar Live Agency Registration Ki Simple Guide (Hindi + English)

Apni khud ki online agency start karke hosts recruit karna aur commission earn karna chahte hain? Yeh raha poora process, simple bhasha mein.

Agency Kya Karti Hai

Ek Sitar Live agency hosts ko recruit, train, manage aur support karti hai, aur team ki eligible weekly income ke hisaab se commission receive karti hai.

Eligibility

  • Kam se kam 18 saal ka hona chahiye
  • Genuine personal information aur verification zaroori hai
  • Hosts ko recruit aur support karne ki ability honi chahiye

Registration Process

  1. Agency-registration support team se contact karein.
  2. Accurate personal aur agency details submit karein.
  3. Verification complete karein aur review ka wait karein.
  4. Approval ke baad hosts recruit karna shuru karein.

Commission

$0–$799 weekly team income par 15%, $800–$1,499 par 20%, aur $1,500+ par 25%. Rates badal sakte hain, isliye latest rules confirm kar lein.

Application se approval guaranteed nahi hoti. Details ke liye Sitar Live Agency page dekhein.
