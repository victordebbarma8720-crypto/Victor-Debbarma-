Aaja ID Kaise Banaye: Step-by-Step Account Creation Guide

"Aaja ID kaise banaye" simply means "how do I create an Aaja ID" — and it's one of the first things people search for once they've decided they want to try hosting or running an agency on Aaja. The good news is that account creation itself is not complicated. It mainly involves reaching out to the right support channel, sharing a few basic details, and going through a short verification step. This guide walks through that process in plain terms, what you'll generally be asked for, and what to expect once your ID is active.

What "Aaja ID Kaise Banaye" Is Asking

At its core, an Aaja ID is your personal account on the platform — the identity tied to your calls, your diamond balance, and your activity history if you're a host, or to your recruited team if you're running an agency. Creating one is the first practical step before you can do anything else on Aaja, which is why it's such a commonly searched question among newcomers.

As Aaja Agency India, we're an independent support service that helps people through this process — we are not the Aaja app itself, and we don't control in-app screens or exact signup flows. What we can offer is reliable guidance on the general steps involved and direct support if you get stuck anywhere along the way.

Before You Start: What to Have Ready

Having a few basics ready before you reach out makes the process noticeably smoother:

  • A smartphone with a stable internet connection
  • A working mobile number you can be reached on
  • Basic identification details for verification, as requested during onboarding
  • A bank account or UPI ID, especially if you're setting up a host account for future withdrawals
  • A decision on whether you want a host ID, an agency ID, or want to understand both before choosing — see our Aaja Agency vs Host comparison if you're unsure

None of this needs to be complicated — it's simply easier to move through the process quickly when you're not scrambling for details halfway through.

Step-by-Step Account Creation Process

At a general level, creating your Aaja ID through our guidance follows these steps:

  1. Step 1 — Reach out to support: Message our team on WhatsApp and let them know you want help creating an Aaja ID.
  2. Step 2 — Share your basic details: Provide your name, contact number, and let us know whether you're interested in a host ID or an agency ID.
  3. Step 3 — Get guided through setup: Our team explains what information you'll need to provide and what the general onboarding flow looks like.
  4. Step 4 — Complete verification: Confirm your details so your account can be activated properly — see the next section for what this usually involves.
  5. Step 5 — Start using your ID: Once active, you can begin exploring calls and tasks as a host, or begin recruiting as an agency.

For more detail on what comes after your ID is created, our Aaja Host Registration Guide and Aaja Agency Registration Guide both go deeper into their respective paths.

What Happens During Verification

Verification exists to confirm you are who you say you are and to keep the platform reasonably safe for everyone using it. In general terms, this usually involves confirming your contact details and providing basic identification information as requested during onboarding. We deliberately keep this description general rather than listing exact screens or fields, since in-app steps can be updated by the platform over time, and we don't want to give you outdated or inaccurate instructions.

If anything about the verification step feels unclear or you're asked for something that doesn't seem right, the safest move is to pause and check directly with our support team before continuing. Our full Aaja Account Verification Guide covers this in more depth.

A short pause to confirm something with support is always better than guessing your way through verification and getting stuck later.

Choosing a Host ID or an Agency ID

Before you create your Aaja ID, it's worth being clear on which type of account you actually want. A host ID is built around going live and taking calls yourself, while an agency ID is built around recruiting and supporting a team of hosts rather than appearing on camera. Both are legitimate starting points, and which one suits you depends more on your personality and available time than any technical difference in the signup process itself.

If you're leaning toward hosting, our Aaja Hosting page covers what that role generally involves. If an agency role sounds more appealing, our Aaja Agency page has the full breakdown of eligibility and responsibilities.

Common Issues While Creating an Aaja ID

A handful of small issues come up repeatedly for people setting up a new Aaja ID, and it helps to know about them in advance:

  • Mismatched contact details. Make sure the number or details you share with support match what you plan to use consistently going forward.
  • Rushing through verification. Taking a few extra minutes to double-check details before submitting them tends to save time overall.
  • Not asking questions early. If something about the process is unclear, it's much easier to ask before your ID is set up than to try to fix confusion afterward.
  • Expecting instant results after setup. Creating your ID is just the starting line — what happens after, in terms of calls, tasks, or recruitment, is where the real work begins.

None of these are serious problems, but avoiding them upfront makes for a noticeably smoother first week.

What to Do After Your ID Is Set Up

Once your Aaja ID is active, the real work shifts from account creation to actually using the platform well. If you've set up a host ID, that typically means getting comfortable with calls, understanding daily tasks, and building a consistent routine — our Aaja Beginner Guide is a good next read. If you've set up an agency ID, your focus shifts to finding and supporting hosts, which our guide on joining Aaja Agency in India covers in more detail.

Either way, having an active Aaja ID is only the first step. What you do with it over the following weeks matters far more than how smoothly the account creation itself went.
