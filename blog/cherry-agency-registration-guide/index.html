Cherry Agency Registration: Step-by-Step Guide

A step-by-step guide to Cherry Agency registration: what to prepare, how the process works and the responsibilities you take on as a Cherry app agency.

By CherryOfficial.in Updated 27 October 2026 6 min read

In this article

  1. What a Cherry agency does
  2. What to prepare
  3. Registration steps
  4. After you are approved
  5. Common mistakes to avoid
  6. FAQs

Cherry Agency registration lets you build and manage a team of hosts on the Cherry app. This guide walks through what to prepare and the steps involved. For an overview, see our Cherry Agency registration page.

What a Cherry agency does

A Cherry agency recruits hosts, helps them register, shares their schedules and supports them in meeting performance targets. The agency is also responsible for making sure its hosts understand and follow Cherry’s rules.

What to prepare

Requirements can change, so confirm the current list with us. Commonly requested items include:

  • Proof that you meet the app’s minimum age requirement
  • Valid identity details for verification, if requested
  • A smartphone with the official Cherry app installed
  • An active WhatsApp number for communicating with your team
  • A basic plan for how you will recruit and support hosts

Registration steps

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 8132958338 and say you want to register a Cherry Agency.
  2. Confirm the requirements we share with you, and ask any questions about terms.
  3. Install the official Cherry app from an official source. See our Cherry app guide.
  4. Complete the application following the steps we provide.
  5. Wait for review. Applications are reviewed through the official process; approval is not guaranteed.

After you are approved

Once approved, your focus shifts to building a reliable team:

  • Recruit hosts honestly and explain the real conditions, including the weekly base salary plan rules.
  • Help each host through Cherry host registration and verification.
  • Share schedules clearly, including the 9 PM–2 AM IST rush-hour window from the supplied plan.
  • Review performance regularly and give practical feedback.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Promising hosts guaranteed income or approval.
  • Recruiting people who do not meet the age or verification rules.
  • Asking hosts for their passwords or login codes.
  • Paying anyone who claims they can guarantee your agency approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Cherry Agency registration take?

It depends on the official review process. We will share what to expect, but timelines and approval are not guaranteed.

Do I need experience to register an agency?

Experience managing people helps, but what matters most is your ability to recruit honestly, communicate clearly and follow the rules.

Can I be both a host and an agency?

Ask us on WhatsApp. Whether this is allowed depends on the current official rules.
