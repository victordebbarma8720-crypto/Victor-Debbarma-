What Is the Video Call Earning App Category?

Video call earning apps are a category of mobile apps where users connect with each other, or with hosts, through live video. Viewers can send virtual gifts during a call, and hosts convert those gifts into an in-app currency — often called diamonds or coins — which can eventually be exchanged for real payouts, subject to each app's own rules and minimums. Many apps in this category also run daily or weekly tasks that reward hosts for staying active, and some offer an additional agency layer where a person can build and support a team of hosts rather than appearing on camera themselves.

This is a broad category with apps that differ in call format, audience, language focus, and how seriously they invest in supporting hosts once they've signed up. We're not going to name or evaluate specific competing apps here — we can't verify claims we haven't tested ourselves, and it wouldn't be fair to make assumptions about products we don't operate. Instead, this guide focuses on how the category generally functions and on what we do know well: Aaja and the registration support we provide through Aaja Agency India.

How This Category Generally Works

Across most apps in this space, the earning mechanics follow a similar pattern:

  • Calls or live sessions — hosts go live or take one-on-one and match calls with viewers.
  • Virtual gifts — viewers can send gifts during a session, which typically convert into points, coins, or diamonds for the host.
  • Tasks and rewards — apps often add daily or weekly tasks that reward consistent activity beyond just gifts, such as call-time targets.
  • Withdrawals — accumulated in-app currency can usually be converted and withdrawn once certain thresholds or verification steps are met.

Aaja follows a broadly similar structure, with its own version of match calls, diamonds, and a task system. For specifics on how this works on Aaja, our Aaja Diamonds Explained and Aaja Daily Tasks Guide cover the mechanics in detail.

Host Programs vs Agency Systems

One feature that distinguishes some apps in this category from simple content platforms is the presence of an agency system. Rather than every host signing up independently, an agency recruits, onboards, and supports a group of hosts, earning a commission tied to the team's combined activity. This structure is common because it helps apps scale their host base through word-of-mouth recruitment, while giving motivated individuals a way to build something bigger than a single personal account.

Aaja uses this same general model. You can join as an individual host, focused purely on your own calls, or as an agency, focused on recruiting and supporting others. What to Look For Before Choosing an App

Whichever app in this category you're considering, a few general questions are worth asking before you invest real time:

  • Does the app have clear, understandable rules around diamonds, gifts, and withdrawals?
  • Is there any kind of support — in your language — if you run into an issue during registration or payouts?
  • Does the call format (one-on-one, match calls, group live) actually suit your comfort level and personality?
  • Is there a realistic path to grow, whether through better host tools, a level system, or an agency option?

These questions apply regardless of which specific app you're looking at, and they're a reasonable filter for sorting out which platforms are worth your time versus which ones leave you guessing.
