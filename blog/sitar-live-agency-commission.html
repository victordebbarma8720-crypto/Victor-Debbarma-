Sitar Live Agency Commission Explained

How Commission Tiers and Sub-Agency Bonuses Work

Agency commission is the main reason applicants start a Sitar Live agency, so it's worth understanding exactly how it's calculated before you apply. This guide breaks down the standard commission tiers and the sub-agency programme in plain terms.

How Base Commission Is Calculated

The stated agency commission is based on the total eligible weekly income generated by the agency's entire host team — not any single host's earnings. As your team's combined weekly income rises, your commission percentage rises with it.

Weekly Host-Team IncomeCommission
$0 – $79915%
$800 – $1,49920%
$1,500 or more25%

Example: if your agency's eligible host team generates $1,000 in a week, the stated commission rate is 20%, meaning $200 in commission for that week.

The Sub-Agency Programme

Eligible senior agents may be allowed to recruit and manage sub-agencies for an additional layer of income. Sub-agent and senior-agent commissions are calculated on the same weekly-income tiers, but at different rates:

Weekly Team IncomeSub-AgentSenior Agent
$0 – $79910%5%
$800 – $1,49912%8%
$1,500 or more15%10%

Example: if a sub-agency generates $1,000 in eligible weekly income, the sub-agent may receive 12% ($120) while the senior agent overseeing them may receive 8% ($80) on top.

Sub-Agency Requirements

The stated policy requires a business-development agent to recruit at least five valid sub-agencies, and each valid sub-agency should have at least ten active hosts to remain eligible for the stated commission structure. These thresholds can change, so applicants should confirm the latest rules before joining.

Commission rates, eligibility rules and calculation methods may change according to current platform policies. Agency income is performance-based and is not guaranteed.
