Sitarlive Agency Registration Guide

Step-by-Step Process to Become an Agency Partner

Sitarlive Agency July 27, 2026 6 min read

Sitarlive Agency Registration is the starting point for anyone who wants to build a team of hosts and earn ongoing weekly commissions. This guide breaks the process down step by step, based on the official Sitarlive Agent Working Policy, so you know exactly what to expect before you begin.

Who Can Register as a Sitarlive Agency?

You don't need prior industry experience to apply. What matters most is your ability to recruit and support hosts, communicate clearly, and manage a small team consistently. Many successful agencies start with just a handful of hosts and grow from there.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

1. Contact Support

Reach out to our team on WhatsApp at +91 8132958338 and let us know you want to complete Sitarlive Agency Registration.

2. Share Your Details

You'll be asked for basic details to set up your agency profile, including your contact information and preferred payout method — Bank Transfer or USDT (TRC20).

3. Get Your Agency Account

Once verified, you'll receive your agency account and access to recruit and onboard hosts under your team.

4. Start Recruiting Hosts

Begin building your team. Recruit hosts through referrals and social channels, and support them through onboarding so they can start earning quickly.

How Agency Commission Works

Once your hosts start earning, your commission is calculated on their combined weekly gift and call income:

  • $0–$799 weekly host income → 15% agent commission
  • $800–$1,499 weekly host income → 20% agent commission
  • $1,500 and above weekly host income → 25% agent commission
Sub-agent bonus: You can also recruit sub-agents. When your sub-agent's team earns, you receive an additional 5%–10% on that income, on top of your own agent commission — provided you maintain at least 5 valid sub-agencies with 10 or more active hosts each.

Weekly Payment Schedule

Agency commissions are paid out every Sunday (UTC+0) via Bank Transfer or USDT (TRC20), with payments completing within 3 working days. The minimum withdrawal is $10, with a 5% bank fee or a USDT fee of more than $2.

Staying Compliant

To keep your agency status active, avoid behavior prohibited under the Sitarlive Agent Working Policy — such as allowing multiple host accounts, withholding host salaries, or requesting off-platform payments. Full details are covered on our Sitarlive Agency page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does registration take?

Registration is typically fast — once you contact support, onboarding can usually be completed within a few days.

Do I need a minimum team size?

No fixed minimum is required to start, though your commission rate rises as your hosts' weekly income grows, and full sub-agent commission requires 5 valid sub-agencies with 10+ active hosts each.
