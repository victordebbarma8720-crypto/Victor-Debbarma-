Sitarlive App Download Guide

Install the App and Register in Minutes

Sitarlive App July 27, 2026 4 min read

If you're looking to start earning with Sitarlive, the first step is getting the Sitarlive App downloaded and set up correctly. This guide walks you through the full process — from installing the app to registering as a host or under a Sitarlive Agency — so you can start going live without delays.

Step 1: Download the Sitarlive App

The Sitarlive App is available for Android devices. Make sure you download it from an official source, and avoid third-party links that are not verified by your agency or Sitarlive support, since unofficial app files can be unsafe.

Step 2: Create Your Account

Open the app and sign up using your phone number or email. Verify your account with the OTP sent to you, then complete your profile with a clear display name and profile photo.

Step 3: Register Through an Agency

To join as a host and be eligible for onboarding support, level progression, and agency guidance, it's best to register through a Sitarlive Agency rather than as a standalone user. Our team can guide you through this step directly on WhatsApp.

  • Faster onboarding and account verification
  • Guidance on going live, calls, and virtual gifts
  • Support with weekly withdrawal requests
  • Help resolving any account issues

Step 4: Set Up Your Payment Details

Before your first withdrawal, add accurate Bank Transfer or USDT (TRC20) details in the app. Double-check every digit — incorrect bank or wallet details will cause your withdrawal request to be rejected, and you'll need to reapply.

Good to know: The minimum withdrawal on Sitarlive is $10 (100,000 coins), and withdrawals are processed every Sunday (UTC+0), completing within 3 working days.

Step 5: Start Going Live

Once your account and payment details are set up, you can start live streaming, taking calls, and receiving virtual gifts. Consistent activity and good engagement help you build a steady weekly income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Sitarlive App free to download?

Yes, downloading and installing the Sitarlive App is completely free. You only need a smartphone and an internet connection.

Do I need an agency to register?

You don't strictly need one, but registering through an official Sitarlive Agency gives you onboarding support, faster issue resolution, and guidance on maximizing your weekly earnings.

What should I prepare before installing the app?

Have a valid phone number or email ready for verification, and your bank account or USDT (TRC20) wallet details ready for future withdrawals.
