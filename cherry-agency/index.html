Cherry Agency Registration: Requirements and Guide

Thinking about Cherry Agency registration? This guide explains what a Cherry agency does, the requirements you should expect, how to register, and the responsibilities that come with managing hosts on the Cherry app.

Apply for Cherry Agency See the steps
Overview

What Is Cherry Agency?

A Cherry Agency is a partner that recruits, onboards and supports hosts on the Cherry app. Hosts take calls with users; the agency helps them get started, explains the rules and targets, and keeps them informed about schedules and changes.

Running a Cherry app agency is a management role. A good agency is honest with hosts about requirements, communicates clearly, and makes sure the team follows Cherry’s policies.

An agency is a good fit if you…

  • Enjoy building and organising a team
  • Can explain rules and targets clearly
  • Respond to hosts’ questions reliably
  • Are comfortable following platform policies strictly
Registration

An Introduction to Agency Registration

Cherry Agency registration is the process of applying to manage hosts on the Cherry app. It usually involves confirming your identity, agreeing to the platform’s terms and setting up how you will manage your team. We guide you through each step on WhatsApp.

Before you start: The requirements below are those commonly requested. Official requirements can change, so we confirm the current list with you before you submit anything.
Eligibility

Cherry Agency Requirements

What you should be ready to provide or show. Please confirm the current requirements with us.

Basic requirements

  • You meet the minimum age required by the Cherry app’s terms (adults only)
  • A smartphone with the official Cherry app installed
  • Valid identity details for verification, if requested
  • An active WhatsApp number for team communication

Practical requirements

  • Time to recruit, onboard and support hosts
  • Ability to explain schedules, rush hours and targets
  • Willingness to follow Cherry policies and local laws
  • A plan to keep your hosts’ personal data private

Responsibilities

Cherry Agency Responsibilities

Registering an agency means taking responsibility for how your team is recruited and supported.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Honest recruitment

Explain requirements, targets and plan conditions accurately. Never promise guaranteed income or approval.

🎓

Onboarding & training

Help new hosts install the official app, complete verification and understand how calls and targets work.

🗓️

Schedules & attendance

Share assigned schedules clearly, including rush hours, and help hosts keep consistent attendance.

📈

Performance support

Help hosts track answer rate and call duration, and give practical feedback when targets are missed.

🛡️

Policy compliance

Make sure every host understands and follows Cherry’s community guidelines and terms of service.

🔒

Privacy & security

Handle hosts’ personal information carefully and never ask anyone to share passwords or login codes.

Policies & compliance

Policies Every Cherry Agency Should Follow

Platform rules

  • Only recruit hosts who meet the app’s minimum age and verification rules
  • Follow Cherry’s terms of service and community guidelines at all times
  • Do not encourage hosts to share contact details or move users off the platform if the rules forbid it
  • Report abuse or rule violations through official channels

Fair and legal practice

  • Give hosts accurate, written information about plan conditions
  • Never take hosts’ account credentials or payments meant for them
  • Respect data-protection and privacy obligations
  • Keep records of your income for tax purposes and follow applicable laws
Be cautious: Treat any offer that guarantees approval or fixed income in exchange for an upfront payment as a warning sign. Ask for terms in writing and confirm them through official Cherry channels.
FAQ

Cherry Agency Registration FAQs

Who can apply for Cherry Agency registration?

Adults who can recruit and manage hosts responsibly and follow Cherry’s rules. Exact eligibility requirements can change, so message us on WhatsApp to confirm the current ones before you apply.

Is there a fee for Cherry Agency registration?

We have not been given any official registration fee to publish. Ask us on WhatsApp for current terms, and be cautious of anyone who asks for money in exchange for guaranteed approval.

How long does Cherry Agency approval take?

Review times vary and are decided through the official Cherry process. We will tell you what to expect based on current information, but we cannot guarantee a timeline or approval.

How does a Cherry agency earn?

Agency compensation depends on the official terms offered at the time you register and on your team’s activity. We do not publish earnings figures we cannot verify; ask us for the current terms in writing.

Can an agency manage hosts in different cities?

Hosts work through the Cherry app, so a team does not have to be in one place. Schedules use India Standard Time in the plan supplied to us, so make sure every host understands their assigned hours.

What is the difference between an agency and a host?

Hosts take calls on the Cherry app. Agencies recruit and support hosts. If you want to take calls yourself, see Cherry host registration.
