Your Role as a Kandy Agency

📈 Income Guide

How Much Can a Kandy Agency Earn in India?

A detailed breakdown of realistic Kandy agency earnings at every stage — from startup to elite tier.

Understanding the Kandy Agency Business Model

The Kandy agency model is built around recruitment, training, and performance management. As an agency owner, you don't stream yourself — you build a team of hosts, support their growth, and earn a percentage of everything they collect. This is a scalable, recurring income model.

Your income grows in direct proportion to how many active, high-performing hosts you manage. A host earning ₹30,000/month with a 30% commission rate contributes ₹9,000 to your income each month — and that number multiplies with every host you add to your roster.

The most successful Kandy agencies in India combine smart recruitment (finding passionate, consistent streamers), ongoing coaching (helping hosts grow their streamer score and hit daily targets), and performance management (tracking dashboards, setting goals, rewarding top performers).

Agency Earnings at Each Stage

🥉 Stage 1: Starter (1–5 Hosts)

With 3–5 active hosts earning an average of ₹15,000/month each, your 20% commission produces ₹9,000–₹15,000/month. This is the learning phase — focus on recruitment and retention.

🥈 Stage 2: Growing (6–20 Hosts)

With 10–15 hosts at 30% commission, agencies typically earn ₹45,000–₹1,50,000/month. At this stage, systematized training and delegation become important.

🥇 Stage 3: Elite (20+ Hosts)

Elite agencies with 30–50+ hosts at 35–40% commission can realistically earn ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000+ per month. These agencies often have dedicated managers per host group.

🎓 What Skills Do You Need to Run a Kandy Agency?

📢 Recruitment

🎓 Training & Coaching
