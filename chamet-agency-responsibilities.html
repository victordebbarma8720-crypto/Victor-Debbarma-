Chamet Agency Responsibilities and Rules

Running an agency means other people rely on what you tell them. This guide sets out the responsibilities every Chamet agency applicant should be prepared for.

In this article

  1. Recruit honestly
  2. Share accurate registration information
  3. Explain the hosting guidelines
  4. Respect privacy and account security
  5. Be clear about commission and payments
  6. Follow the applicable platform rules
  7. A simple code of conduct

Recruit honestly

The most important rule for any agency is to be honest with potential hosts. Explain what hosting involves, what the current requirements are and what is not guaranteed. Avoid phrases such as “guaranteed income” or “earn ₹X per day”. Earnings depend on eligible activity and platform rules, and misleading promises damage trust and may break platform rules.

Share accurate registration information

Point hosts to authorised registration channels and official app listings. Do not circulate unverified links, and do not ask hosts to pay to register.

Explain the hosting guidelines

New hosts often do not read the rules in detail. A good agency explains the key content and conduct guidelines, profile requirements and verification steps, and encourages hosts to read the official policies themselves.

Respect privacy and account security

  • Never ask a host for their password or verification codes.
  • Do not log in to a host’s account on their behalf.
  • Store any personal information you receive securely and only for as long as needed.
  • Do not share a host’s personal details, photos or earnings with others.

Be clear about commission and payments

Hosts should understand how their own payments work, and you should understand how your commission is calculated. If you are unsure, check the current terms before answering questions. Our commission explainer covers the general formula.

Follow the applicable platform rules

Agency agreements and platform terms set out what agencies may and may not do. Read them, keep a copy and follow them. Where this guide and the official terms differ, the official terms apply.

A simple code of conduct

  1. Tell the truth about requirements and earnings.
  2. Use only authorised registration channels.
  3. Protect host privacy and account security.
  4. Explain the rules, and follow them yourself.
  5. Never charge hosts fees for registration.
