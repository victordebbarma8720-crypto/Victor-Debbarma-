What is Masti Hosting?

What is Masti Hosting?

A Masti host is a content creator or entertainer who goes live, accepts video calls, and interacts with users on the Masti App. As a host, you earn Beans — the platform's virtual currency — from every minute of calls, gifts sent by users, and match interactions.

The Masti Anchor App is designed to make the hosting experience smooth and rewarding. Hosts are supported by their agencies, who help them onboard, train, and improve their performance on the platform.

Masti Anchor registration is open to individuals across India. To get started, you download the Masti App, complete your registration, and join an active Masti Agency that will guide you through the initial setup and approval process.

💡 Before You Start

One person can operate only one Masti account. Always work in a quiet, well-lit space. Maintain a professional and polite attitude with users. Do not share your personal contact details through the platform, and never participate in offline payment arrangements.

Weekly Bonus Program

👑
A Level Host

Agency Bonus: For every S Level host who completes weekly tasks, the managing agency also receives a $3 bonus. For every A Level host, the agency earns a $2 bonus — in addition to their standard commission.

Platform Rules

Masti Anchor Host Rules

All Masti hosts are expected to follow these rules at all times. Violation of platform rules can lead to warnings, reduced earnings, or account suspension.

👤

One account per person. One person can operate only one Masti account. Multiple accounts are strictly prohibited and lead to permanent suspension.

🚫

Do not share personal contact details. Never share your phone number, social media profiles, or any personal contact information through the Masti App.

💰

Offline payments are not allowed. All transactions must happen through the official Masti platform. Accepting offline payments or gifts in any form is a serious violation.

📸

Maintain a neat and professional appearance. Present yourself professionally during video calls and live streams. Your appearance directly impacts user retention and gift income.

🔇

Work in a quiet place. Background noise significantly reduces call quality and user experience. Always host from a quiet, distraction-free environment.

📻

Use stable internet connection. Unstable internet leads to call drops, poor streaming quality, and lower call ratings, which affects your bean earnings.

🤗

Communicate politely with all users. Always be respectful and polite. Rude or dismissive behaviour towards users can result in complaints and account warnings.

📞

Do not ignore user calls or messages. Maintaining a call answer rate above 90% and text response rate above 80% is required for weekly task completion and bonus eligibility.

🚧

Avoid abusive or inappropriate content. Any content that violates community guidelines, including abusive language, inappropriate behaviour, or adult content, is strictly forbidden and will result in immediate action.

Important Withdrawal Notes

✓

Masti supports self-withdrawal directly through the Masti App. You do not need to go through your agency to withdraw your earnings.

✓

The minimum withdrawal amount is $5 USD. You cannot withdraw amounts below this threshold.

✓

All withdrawal amounts must be in exact multiples of $5. For example: $5, $10, $15, $20, and so on.

Daily withdrawals are allowed, so you can cash out your earnings every day once your balance meets the minimum requirement.

⚠

If a refund is processed on your account, the refund amount may be deducted from your related host earnings. Always maintain fair interactions with users to avoid refund situations.

FAQ

Masti Host Registration – Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a Masti host?+
To register as a Masti host, download the Masti App or Masti Anchor App, create your account, and connect with a Masti Agency. The agency will guide you through the activation and onboarding process so you can start accepting calls and earning beans.
What is the difference between Masti App and Masti Anchor App?+
The Masti App is the main user platform where users browse and connect with hosts. The Masti Anchor App is specifically designed for hosts and agencies to manage their profiles, track earnings, and monitor performance metrics. Both are available for download.
How many hours should a Masti host work daily?+
For weekly task completion and bonus eligibility, hosts should work a minimum of 240 minutes (4 hours) total per day, with at least 120 minutes during the designated peak hours (9 PM–1 AM IST). Working 5 days per week is required to qualify for the weekly bonus.
Can a Masti host earn without an agency?+
On the Masti platform, host activation typically requires going through a registered Masti Agency. The agency supports you through the registration, onboarding, and approval process. Without an agency, the registration and activation process may be more difficult.
What happens if I miss my weekly tasks?+
If you do not complete your weekly tasks, you will not qualify for the weekly bonus ($10 or $15 depending on your level). Consistent underperformance may also affect your host level, which in turn reduces your weekly bonus and your agency's commission tier.
Is Masti hosting available for both men and women?+
Yes, the Masti Anchor App registration is open to both men and women in India. As long as you meet the platform's requirements and follow all host rules, you can join as a Masti host regardless of gender.
