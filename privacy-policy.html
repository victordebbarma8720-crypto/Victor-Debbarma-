Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Introduction

SitarliveAgency.in ("we", "us", "our") is an independent registration and onboarding support website for people interested in Sitarlive host and agency opportunities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit this website or contact our support team.

Information We Collect

We may collect limited information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us, including:

  • Your name and contact number when you message us on WhatsApp
  • Basic details you share for host or agency registration assistance
  • General usage data such as pages visited, browser type and device type, collected automatically through standard website analytics

How We Use Your Information

Information you share with us is used only to:

  • Respond to your registration or support enquiry
  • Provide guidance on host or agency onboarding
  • Improve the accuracy and usefulness of our website content

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp Communication

When you contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms of service, in addition to this policy. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy practices separately.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand how visitors use our pages and to improve website performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

Our website may reference or link to third-party platforms, including the Sitarlive App. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

Your Choices

You may choose not to share certain information with us. However, this may limit our ability to assist you with registration or onboarding guidance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
