Privacy Policy

How HollahOfficial.com collects, uses and protects information from visitors and applicants.

This policy applies to HollahOfficial.com, an independent Hollah host and agency registration and onboarding support service. It does not apply to the Hollah app itself, which has its own separate privacy policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, WhatsApp number, enquiry type and message, when you use our contact form or message us directly.

Contact-Form Information

Our contact form is designed to open WhatsApp with your details pre-filled so you can send them directly to our support number. As a static website, the form does not transmit or store your information on a server or database.

WhatsApp Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp, your messages and phone number are handled according to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, in addition to how our team uses that information to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand general site usage and improve content. Any such tools operate according to their own respective privacy policies.

How Information Is Used

Information you share with us is used only to respond to your enquiry, provide registration guidance and improve our support service.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to handle your information responsibly. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

We may reference or link to third-party services such as WhatsApp or official app stores. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including the official Hollah app or app store listings. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

User Rights

You may request that we stop contacting you or delete information you have shared with us by messaging our WhatsApp support number.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to provide support, unless you request earlier deletion.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of this website after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Method
