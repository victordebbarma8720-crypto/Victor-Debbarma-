Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website

This privacy policy explains what information sitarliveofficial.com ("we", "us") collects from visitors and applicants, how it is used, and how it is protected. This website is an independent registration and onboarding support platform and is not the official Sitar Live platform.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through the WhatsApp button, contact form, or email, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and any message details related to Sitar Live host or agency registration.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your registration or support enquiry
  • To provide guidance on host or agency registration, eligibility and onboarding
  • To communicate with you about the status of your enquiry

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for and you should never share passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, banking PINs, card PINs, wallet private keys, or recovery codes through this website, WhatsApp, or any communication with us or anyone claiming to represent us.

Third-Party Platforms

Contacting us via WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We do not control how third-party platforms, including Sitar Live itself, handle information submitted directly to them during app registration or verification.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as necessary to respond to your request and provide onboarding support, after which it may be deleted.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete information you've shared with us by contacting us at the details below. This does not affect any information you have separately submitted directly to Sitar Live.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Please review it periodically for any changes.
