📋 Requirements

Kandy Host Eligibility & Requirements

Kandy welcomes hosts from all backgrounds. The requirements are minimal and designed to ensure a quality, safe streaming environment.

✅ Basic Requirements

  • Age 18 years or older
  • Valid Indian mobile number
  • Android (5.0+) or iOS (11+) smartphone
  • Stable internet connection (4G/WiFi)
  • Front camera for live streaming
  • Willingness to stream regularly (minimum 1 hr/day)
  • Adherence to Kandy community guidelines

⭐ Tips for New Hosts

  • Stream at consistent, fixed timings each day
  • Greet viewers by name to build personal connections
  • Use good lighting — natural light works perfectly
  • Engage with comments and gift givers actively
  • Set daily targets and announce them to viewers
  • Collaborate with other hosts for cross-promotion
  • Participate in Kandy events for extra visibility

🆓 Zero Investment Required

Becoming a Kandy host requires absolutely zero investment. The app is free to download, registration is free, and joining our agency is free. You only need your time, your personality, and a device to stream from.
