FAQ

Quick answers

What is Kamaichat Official?

Kamaichat Official is a host and agency recruitment platform for India. We help people join as live hosts who earn by interacting with users through live video, voice and chat, and we help agencies onboard, train and manage their own host teams.

Is registration really free?

Yes. Registration for both hosts and agencies is 100% free. Kamaichat Official never asks for a registration fee, security deposit, or your OTP/password. If anyone asks you for money in our name, please report it to us on WhatsApp.

Who can join Kamaichat?

Anyone aged 18 or above living in India, with a smartphone, a stable internet connection and a valid government ID can apply as a host. Agencies can be individuals, teams or registered businesses who can recruit and support hosts.

How much can a host earn?

Earnings depend on your activity, hours online, consistency and the platform's current reward policies. There is no fixed salary and no guaranteed income — hosts who are regular, friendly and professional generally earn more. Full, up-to-date details are shared during onboarding.

Can I host part-time or from home?

Yes. Hosting is flexible — you choose your own hours and host from home using your phone. Many hosts are students, homemakers and working professionals doing it part-time.
