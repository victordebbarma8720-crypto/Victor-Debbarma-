About Kamaichat Official

“Kamai” means earning. We built Kamaichat Official to give everyday people across India a simple, honest way to earn by doing what they enjoy — talking, entertaining and connecting.

Our story

Opportunity for every town, every language

Live streaming has created a new way to earn — but many talented people in India don't know where to begin, who to trust, or how to grow. Kamaichat Official exists to close that gap.

We connect aspiring hosts with clear information, guide them through registration and verification, and support agencies that recruit and mentor hosts in their own communities. From metro cities to the North-East and small towns, we welcome everyone who is 18+, respectful and ready to learn.

Our mission

To help one lakh Indians discover a flexible, home-based way to earn through live hosting — with honesty, safety and real human support at every step.

What we stand for
