What hosts do

Be yourself, be live, be rewarded

A Kamaichat host goes live on a social live-streaming and chat platform to talk, entertain and connect with users. Viewers can follow you, chat with you and send virtual gifts — which turn into rewards for you according to the platform's policy.

  • Chat & conversation — friendly talk, advice, daily life and fun.
  • Talent shows — singing, dancing, poetry, mimicry, cooking and more.
  • Language & culture — host in your mother tongue and build a loyal regional audience.

Host eligibility checklist

  • 18 years or older
  • Valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or DL)
  • Smartphone with front camera and good lighting
  • Stable Wi-Fi or 4G/5G connection
  • Bank account or UPI in your own name
  • Friendly, respectful and consistent

Success tips

Tips from experienced hosts

  • Be regular. Go live at the same times every day so viewers know when to find you.
  • Look and sound clear. Face a window or a ring light and use earphones with a mic.
  • Greet everyone by name. Viewers love feeling noticed — it builds loyalty.
  • Keep it respectful. Follow community guidelines; never share personal contact details or meet strangers.
  • Plan your content. Songs, quizzes, stories and Q&A keep sessions lively.

Honest earnings information: Kamaichat does not promise a fixed salary. Earnings depend on your activity, online hours, audience engagement and the platform's current reward policy. We share the latest, complete details during onboarding — before you start.

Stay safe: Registration is free. We will never ask you for money, your OTP, passwords or banking PIN. Report anyone who does.

FAQ

Host questions

Do I need experience to become a host?

No. Many of our hosts start with zero experience. We share onboarding guidance, profile tips and best practices so you can begin confidently.

How much can a host earn?

Earnings depend on your activity, hours online, consistency and the platform's current reward policies. There is no fixed salary and no guaranteed income — hosts who are regular, friendly and professional generally earn more. Full, up-to-date details are shared during onboarding.

Can I host part-time or from home?

Yes. Hosting is flexible — you choose your own hours and host from home using your phone. Many hosts are students, homemakers and working professionals doing it part-time.

How are payments made?

Payment methods, schedules and minimum withdrawal limits follow the platform's current policy and are explained to you during onboarding. Always withdraw only to your own verified bank account or UPI ID.

What documents do I need?

Usually a valid government photo ID (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or Driving Licence) for age and identity verification, plus a clear profile photo. Never share your OTP, passwords or banking PIN with anyone.
