Responsibilities

What a good agency does

  • Recruit genuine hosts aged 18+ who are interested and committed.
  • Help with onboarding — profile photos, bios, ID verification and first lives.
  • Teach the rules so every host follows community guidelines and stays safe.
  • Motivate & track your team's activity and help them improve consistently.
  • Be the first point of support and escalate issues to the Kamaichat team quickly.

FAQ

Agency questions

What does an agency partner do?

An agency partner recruits new hosts, helps them set up quality profiles, guides them on rules and best practices, motivates them to stay active and acts as their first point of support.

How do agencies earn?

Agencies typically earn a commission linked to the performance of the hosts in their team. Exact commission structures follow the platform's current policy and are shared with you in detail during agency onboarding.

Do I need an office or a company to start an agency?

No. You can start as an individual working from home with a smartphone. As your team grows, you can expand into a registered business if you wish.

Is there a minimum number of hosts required?

Requirements can change with platform policy. Contact us on WhatsApp and our team will explain the current requirements and help you plan your first hosts.

Ready to lead your own host team?

Register free as a Kamaichat agency partner. Our agency team will contact you on WhatsApp to get you started.
