Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about joining Kamaichat Official as a host or agency partner. Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

General

What is Kamaichat Official?

Kamaichat Official is a host and agency recruitment platform for India. We help people join as live hosts who earn by interacting with users through live video, voice and chat, and we help agencies onboard, train and manage their own host teams.

Is registration really free?

Yes. Registration for both hosts and agencies is 100% free. Kamaichat Official never asks for a registration fee, security deposit, or your OTP/password. If anyone asks you for money in our name, please report it to us on WhatsApp.

Who can join Kamaichat?

Anyone aged 18 or above living in India, with a smartphone, a stable internet connection and a valid government ID can apply as a host. Agencies can be individuals, teams or registered businesses who can recruit and support hosts.

Which languages are supported?

You can host in Hindi, English, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and other Indian languages. Our support team also helps you in Hindi and English.

How do I contact the Kamaichat team?

The fastest way is WhatsApp on +91 81329 58338. You can also email contact@kamaichatofficial.in or use the form on our Contact page. We reply Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 8 PM IST.

For Hosts

Do I need experience to become a host?

No. Many of our hosts start with zero experience. We share onboarding guidance, profile tips and best practices so you can begin confidently.

How much can a host earn?

Earnings depend on your activity, hours online, consistency and the platform's current reward policies. There is no fixed salary and no guaranteed income — hosts who are regular, friendly and professional generally earn more. Full, up-to-date details are shared during onboarding.

Can I host part-time or from home?

Yes. Hosting is flexible — you choose your own hours and host from home using your phone. Many hosts are students, homemakers and working professionals doing it part-time.

How are payments made?

Payment methods, schedules and minimum withdrawal limits follow the platform's current policy and are explained to you during onboarding. Always withdraw only to your own verified bank account or UPI ID.

What documents do I need?

Usually a valid government photo ID (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or Driving Licence) for age and identity verification, plus a clear profile photo. Never share your OTP, passwords or banking PIN with anyone.

For Agencies

What does an agency partner do?

An agency partner recruits new hosts, helps them set up quality profiles, guides them on rules and best practices, motivates them to stay active and acts as their first point of support.

How do agencies earn?

Agencies typically earn a commission linked to the performance of the hosts in their team. Exact commission structures follow the platform's current policy and are shared with you in detail during agency onboarding.

Do I need an office or a company to start an agency?

No. You can start as an individual working from home with a smartphone. As your team grows, you can expand into a registered business if you wish.

Is there a minimum number of hosts required?

Requirements can change with platform policy. Contact us on WhatsApp and our team will explain the current requirements and help you plan your first hosts.
