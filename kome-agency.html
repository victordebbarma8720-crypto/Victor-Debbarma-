About Kome Agency

What is a Kome Agency & How Does It Work?

A Kome Agency is a certified partner business on the Kome Live platform that operates as a talent recruitment and management entity. As a Kome Agency owner, your primary role is to recruit individual hosts, onboard them onto the Kome Live platform, and support their growth and performance.

In return for recruiting and managing hosts, the Kome Live platform pays your agency a commission based on the performance and earnings of your host team. The larger and more active your host team, the higher your weekly commission income grows.

Kome Agency India helps you register as an official Kome Agency partner, provides recruitment resources, training materials, and ongoing support to help your agency business grow and scale successfully.

Exponential Growth

Sub-Agent Development — Scale Your Agency Beyond Limits

The most successful Kome Agencies don't just recruit hosts — they develop sub-agents who recruit hosts on their behalf. This multiplier effect creates exponential agency growth.

👤

What is a Sub-Agent?

A sub-agent is a person you train to recruit and manage hosts on your behalf. They operate under your agency umbrella, and their recruited hosts contribute to your overall agency performance and commission.

🌳

Why Develop Sub-Agents?

Instead of you recruiting every host personally, sub-agents multiply your reach. While you focus on growing the agency, your sub-agents are actively recruiting new hosts from their own networks simultaneously.

💰

Commission Structure

Your agency earns commission from all hosts — whether you recruited them directly or through sub-agents. As your sub-agent network grows, so does your overall host count and weekly commission income.

Growth Strategies

Proven Kome Agency Growth Strategies

These strategies have been used by the most successful Kome Agency owners to rapidly grow their host teams and maximize weekly commission income.

🎯

Target the Right Candidates

Focus your recruitment on individuals who are communicative, enthusiastic, and have flexible schedules. Passionate people with time to stream regularly outperform reluctant recruits every time.

📊

Monitor Performance Actively

Track each host's streaming hours, earnings, and audience metrics weekly. Identify underperformers early and provide targeted coaching before they drop off the platform.

🏆

Motivate with Competitions

Run internal agency competitions — top earning host of the week, most consistent streamer of the month. Friendly competition motivates hosts to stream more and perform better.

📱

Build an Agency WhatsApp Group

Create a private WhatsApp group for all your agency hosts. Share tips, celebrate wins, coordinate streaming schedules, and build a community culture within your agency.

🔄

Never Stop Recruiting

Host turnover is normal in live streaming. Always have a recruitment pipeline. Set a weekly goal — e.g., speak to 5 new candidates every week — to keep your team growing continuously.

📚

Invest in Host Training

Hosts who receive proper training earn more. More host earnings = more agency commission. Invest time in training your hosts and your commission income grows accordingly.
