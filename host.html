Eligibility

Who Can Apply as a Host?

Sitarlive welcomes new hosts of all backgrounds. Check the basic requirements below before you begin registration.

  • Android smartphone with a stable internet connection
  • Valid and active mobile number for verification
  • Minimum age as required by the Sitarlive app's terms
  • Willingness to go live and interact with viewers regularly
