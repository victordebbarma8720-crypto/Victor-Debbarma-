DarlingC Host Registration

DarlingC host registration may be suitable for eligible women aged 18 or above who want to participate in approved video calls, audio calls, messages, virtual gifts and other platform activities.

Applicants must submit genuine personal information, recent photos and self-recorded verification videos.

How to Complete DarlingC Host Registration

  1. Download the DarlingC app from an official or verified source.
  2. Register using a phone number or Google account.
  3. Select the correct gender.
  4. Complete username, location and date of birth.
  5. Create a secure password.
  6. Open the Host Privileges or V Host section.
  7. Enter the agency code.
  8. Complete face verification.
  9. Upload genuine profile photos.
  10. Upload a self-recorded verification video.
  11. Review the information.
  12. Submit the application.

After approval, a V Host symbol or similar badge may appear on the profile.

Registration steps may change after app updates.
