Introduction to Chamet Agency

Interested in becoming a Chamet agency partner?

This guide explains the general application process, agency responsibilities, host recruitment, commission calculations, and important requirements to review before applying. It is general information from an independent website — always confirm the current terms through authorised channels.

What Is a Chamet Agency?

A Chamet agency arrangement may allow eligible partners to recruit, onboard and support hosts under the applicable platform program. In return, an approved agency may be eligible for commission calculated on eligible activity, according to the current terms.

An agency is a management role. It typically involves explaining registration steps and rules to hosts, helping them prepare, and supporting them responsibly. The exact responsibilities, rights and payment terms depend on the program and agreement that apply when you register.

Chamet Agency Registration Requirements

Specific requirements can change, so treat the following as general preparation steps rather than an official list. Before applying, you should:

  • Review current eligibility criteria — age, identity, region and any activity expectations.
  • Provide accurate information — use details that match any documents you may be asked to verify.
  • Complete required verification — identity or account checks requested by the platform.
  • Accept the applicable terms — read the agency agreement and platform terms before you accept them.
  • Use an authorised registration channel — confirm the channel is genuine before submitting anything.
  • Follow recruitment policies — understand what agencies may and may not do when recruiting hosts.
  • Understand payment conditions — how commission is reported, calculated and paid.

How to Register for a Chamet Agency

  1. Review the agency program

    Understand what the current program offers and requires.

  2. Verify the registration channel

    Confirm that the channel you will use is authorised.

  3. Submit the application

    Provide complete and accurate information.

  4. Complete verification

    Finish any identity or account checks requested.

  5. Follow onboarding instructions

    Use only the authorised onboarding procedure.

  6. Access the management system

    If approved, learn the authorised management tool.

  7. Recruit and support hosts

    Recruit eligible hosts honestly and support them responsibly.

  8. Review commission & payments

    Check how commission and payments work under your terms.

Understanding Chamet Agency Commission

Commission eligibility and calculations depend on the applicable program, the eligible commissionable revenue, the applicable rate and the current terms. In general terms:

Eligible commission = Eligible commissionable revenue × Applicable commission rate

Commission rates may change, and commission is not guaranteed. What counts as “eligible revenue” and which rate applies are defined by the program, not by this website.

Direct-Host and Sub-Agency Commission Concepts

Some agency programs distinguish between commission related to hosts an agency recruits directly and commission related to sub-agencies recruited by that agency. Whether sub-agency arrangements are available, and how any related commission is calculated, depends on the specific authorised program. Do not assume that sub-agency commission follows a particular formula or rate.

Agency Responsibilities

  • Responsible recruitment — recruit only eligible hosts and explain the real requirements.
  • Privacy — never ask for passwords or verification codes; protect hosts’ personal information.
  • Accurate communication — share current, correct information and authorised channels only.
  • Compliance — follow the applicable platform terms and agency agreement.
  • No misleading earnings claims — host income depends on eligible activity and platform rules.

See Chamet agency responsibilities and rules for more detail.

Common Registration Mistakes

  • Using an unverified registration link shared in a chat group.
  • Paying a fee to someone promising guaranteed approval.
  • Relying on undated commission tables or screenshots.
  • Submitting inaccurate or inconsistent details.
  • Promising hosts specific earnings during recruitment.
  • Accepting terms without reading them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register a Chamet agency?

Review the current program, verify that the registration channel is authorised, submit accurate information, complete verification and follow the authorised onboarding procedure.

What requirements should I check?

Check eligibility criteria, identity and verification requirements, regional availability, the agency agreement, recruitment policies and payment conditions.

How is agency commission calculated?

In general terms, eligible commission equals eligible commissionable revenue multiplied by the applicable commission rate. The definitions and rates depend on the current program terms.

Can I recruit sub-agencies?

That depends on the specific authorised program. Check the current terms; do not assume sub-agency arrangements or commission are available.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Applying does not guarantee approval.

Is agency income guaranteed?

No. Commission depends on eligible activity and the applicable program rules.

How can I verify the registration process?

Use information from the official app, the platform’s published documentation or support channels. Be cautious of links that ask for fees, passwords or verification codes.
