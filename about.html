About Aaja Agency India

Honest Guidance for Aaja Hosts and Agencies

Aaja Agency India is an independent support platform built to make Aaja Host Registration and Aaja Agency Registration simple, clear, and stress-free for applicants across India.

Our Story

Why We Started

Aaja is a live video call app that connects hosts with viewers through 1-on-1 video calls, match calls, and virtual gifting. As Aaja grew in popularity, we noticed that many people interested in becoming a host or starting an agency struggled to find clear, accurate information — eligibility rules, task systems, diamond conversion, and the withdrawal process were often scattered or confusing.

Aaja Agency India was created to close that gap. We are a dedicated registration and onboarding support resource — not the official Aaja app or company — focused entirely on helping eligible applicants understand the process and get started correctly, whether they want to host video calls themselves or build a team as an agency partner.
