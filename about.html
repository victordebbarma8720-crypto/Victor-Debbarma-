About ChingariLive.com

ChingariLive.com was created to fill a genuine need: thousands of people across India want to join Chingari as hosts or agency partners but struggle to find clear, reliable guidance on how to do so.

We are a dedicated team of digital marketing and creator economy professionals who saw this gap and built a platform specifically to help individuals navigate the Chingari registration and onboarding journey — completely free of charge.

Our mission is to make Chingari host and agency registration accessible to everyone across India, regardless of their location, prior experience, or background.

  • 🇮🇳 India-based support team with local knowledge
  • 💬 WhatsApp-first support for maximum accessibility
  • 🆓 Completely free registration guidance
  • 🔒 Transparent and ethical platform operations
  • 📝 Clear disclosure of independent status
  • ✅ Community-focused approach to creator support
