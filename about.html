Our Story

Why We Started

We started Clive Agency India because we saw an opportunity that most Indians were missing. The CLIVE platform was growing rapidly and paying real, substantial money to its hosts — but the registration process was unfamiliar to most people, and there was no trusted local partner to guide them through it.

We saw students who needed income but couldn't commit to traditional part-time jobs. Homemakers who wanted financial independence but needed flexible hours. Entrepreneurs who had the drive to build a business but needed a scalable, low-investment model. CLIVE's platform was the answer to all of these — and we became the bridge.

Today, we've helped thousands of Indians register as CLIVE hosts and agency partners. Seeing a homemaker in a small town earn ₹40,000 in her first month, or watching a college student pay his own fees through CLIVE — those are the stories that fuel everything we do. We're not just a recruitment platform. We're a movement for financial freedom.
