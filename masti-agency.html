Understanding Masti Agency

What is a Masti App Agency?

A Masti App Agency is an individual or business that acts as a recruiter and manager for Masti hosts on the Masti platform. The agency is responsible for finding new hosts, helping them register, training them on platform rules, and supporting them to meet weekly task requirements.

In return, the Masti Agency earns a commission percentage based on the total bean income generated by all the hosts under its management. The more active and high-performing hosts you have, the higher your commission tier — and your earnings — will be.

Masti Anchor Agency registration is particularly popular in India because it offers a scalable way to build an online income stream without hosting yourself. You can manage multiple hosts and grow your agency to earn up to 40% commission.

💡 Key Points to Know

A Masti Agency does not earn from Masti's platform revenue directly. Your commission comes from the beans earned by your hosts. The more your hosts earn, the more your agency earns — making host support and training critical to your agency's success.

📈

Commission-Based Income

Earn 10%–40% of your hosts' total bean earnings, paid weekly.

🌟

Weekly Bonuses

Earn $2–$3 bonus for each S or A Level host who completes weekly tasks.

👥

Sub-Agency Network

Expand your Masti Anchor Agency by building a sub-agency network for additional earnings.

Step-by-Step

How to Start a Masti Anchor Agency

Follow these steps to understand the Masti Agency registration and setup process.

1

Download Masti App

Download the Masti App or Masti Anchor App on your Android or iOS device to begin your agency journey.

2

Register Your Account

Create your personal Masti account with accurate details. Your account is the foundation of your agency identity on the platform.

3

Apply for Agency Status

Go to the agency section in the Masti App and submit your application for Masti Agency registration. You may need to meet certain minimum requirements.

4

Wait for Approval

The Masti team reviews agency applications. Once approved, you receive your agency code which you can share with potential Masti hosts.

5

Recruit Masti Hosts

Share your agency code with people interested in becoming Masti hosts. When they register using your code, they become part of your agency.

6

Train and Support Hosts

Guide your hosts on platform rules, peak working hours, weekly task requirements, and how to maximise their bean earnings.

Requirements

Masti Agency Requirements

Before applying for Masti Agency registration, make sure you are aware of these general requirements and expectations.

📱

Active Masti Account

You must have an active and verified account on the Masti App before applying for agency status.

👥

Minimum Host Numbers

The Masti platform may require you to have a minimum number of active hosts before granting full agency commission benefits.

📈

Performance Standards

Your agency is expected to maintain a certain level of host activity and performance to retain your agency status and commission tier.

Active Communication

A good Masti Agency stays in regular contact with its hosts, tracks their weekly task progress, and provides guidance for improvement.

⏳

Consistency

Agency performance is evaluated on a monthly basis. Consistent host activity is key to maintaining and upgrading your commission slab.

Important Warnings

Common Mistakes New Masti Agencies Should Avoid

Learning from common mistakes can save your agency from early setbacks and protect your hosts from rule violations.

❌

Charging hosts a registration fee. Registration on Masti is free. Charging hosts any fee is not only wrong but may violate Masti's terms.

❌

Allowing offline payments between users and hosts. Offline payments are strictly prohibited on the Masti platform and can lead to account suspension.

❌

Neglecting host training. Hosts who do not understand the platform rules, peak hours, or weekly tasks will underperform, directly reducing your agency commission.

❌

Recruiting too many inactive hosts. Focus on quality over quantity. A few active, high-earning hosts are far more valuable than a large number of inactive accounts.

❌

Ignoring call answer rate requirements. Hosts must maintain a call answer rate above 90%. If your hosts are frequently ignoring calls, your agency's performance metrics will suffer.

❌

Sharing personal contact details through the platform. Hosts and agencies should never share personal phone numbers or social media profiles through the Masti App.

FAQ

Masti Agency Registration – Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a Masti Agency in India?+
To register a Masti Agency, first download the Masti App and create a personal account. Then navigate to the agency section and apply for agency registration. You will need to meet minimum platform requirements before your agency is fully activated.
How much can a Masti Agency earn per month?+
Masti Agency earnings depend entirely on how many hosts you have and how much they earn. Commission ranges from 10% to 40% of total host earnings. Additionally, you earn $2–$3 per S or A Level host per week through the weekly bonus program.
What is the difference between Masti Agency and Masti Sub-Agency?+
A Masti Agency directly manages hosts and earns commission on their bean earnings. A sub-agency is a smaller agency that operates under a main agency. The main agency earns an additional sub-agency commission based on the total beans generated by all hosts under its sub-agencies.
Is Masti Agency registration free?+
Yes, applying for Masti Agency status is free through the Masti App. You do not need to pay any registration fee. Beware of anyone claiming otherwise.
Can I manage Masti hosts from anywhere in India?+
Yes. Your Masti Agency is fully remote. You can recruit, manage, and track your hosts from anywhere in India through the Masti App. There is no requirement for a physical office or location.
What happens to my agency if my hosts become inactive?+
If your hosts become inactive, your total bean earnings will fall, which will lower your commission slab. This is why consistent host training, support, and motivation are essential for maintaining a high-earning Masti Agency.
