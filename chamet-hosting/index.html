Introduction

Interested in becoming a Chamet host? This guide explains the general registration process, eligibility and verification, profile preparation, hosting standards, the factors that affect earnings, illustrative Beans conversion examples and the payment points to verify. It is independent, general information — always check the current rules in the official app or through authorised channels.

What Is Chamet Hosting?

Chamet is a video-chat and social app. Hosts on platforms of this kind typically interact with users through video calls or live sessions and may receive virtual gifts, which are credited as an in-app balance. How that balance is valued and paid depends on the current program and platform rules.

Chamet Host Registration Process

  1. Review eligibility

    Check the current host requirements, including age and verification.

  2. Find an authorised channel

    Use an official app listing and a verified registration channel.

  3. Submit accurate information

    Use correct details that match any documents you verify.

  4. Complete verification

    Finish any identity or face verification requested.

  5. Prepare your profile

    Set up a clear, rule-compliant profile.

  6. Review rules & payments

    Read the hosting rules and understand the payment conditions.

Eligibility and Verification

Eligibility rules are set by the platform and can change. Points to check include minimum age (platforms of this kind normally require hosts to be adults), identity or face verification, regional availability and whether registration is direct or through an agency. Complete verification only inside the official app or through an authorised channel.

Profile Preparation

  • Clear profile photos — recent, recognisable and compliant with the content rules.
  • Suitable lighting — face a window or soft light source.
  • Appropriate background — tidy and neutral, with no personal information visible.
  • Accurate description — short, friendly and honest.

More tips: Preparing a professional Chamet host profile.

Hosting Standards

Good hosting is built on respectful communication, account privacy and compliance with the applicable content rules. Read the platform’s community guidelines before you start and follow them consistently. Consequences for breaking rules are decided by the platform under its own policies.

Understanding Host Earnings

Host earnings can depend on many factors, including eligible activity, the virtual gifts received, account status, the program rules that apply and other platform conditions. Because of this, no daily, weekly or monthly income can be guaranteed. Be cautious of anyone who promises a fixed amount.

Payment and Withdrawal Information

Before relying on any balance, verify the following through the official app or authorised documentation:

  • Minimum withdrawal amount.
  • Permitted withdrawal increments.
  • Verification requirements.
  • Supported payment methods.
  • Wallet or account linking requirements.
  • Processing times.
  • Fees and adjustments.
  • Account eligibility.
Historical, unverified notes: older material mentions a $10 minimum withdrawal, withdrawals in multiples of $10, balance carryover, face verification and a wallet called Metwallet. We have not confirmed any of these as current requirements — check the current rules before relying on them.

See the payment and withdrawal guide for a full checklist.

Account Safety and Hosting Rules

  • Never share your password or one-time verification codes — not even with an agency.
  • Link only payment accounts in your own name.
  • Keep personal details such as your address and phone number private.
  • Install the app only from official app store listings.
  • Follow the platform’s content and conduct rules at all times.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a Chamet host?

Review the current eligibility rules, use an official app listing and an authorised registration channel, submit accurate information, complete verification and prepare a compliant profile.

How much can a Chamet host earn?

Earnings vary according to eligible activity, virtual gifts received, account status and the applicable platform rules. No specific income can be guaranteed.

What is the value of Beans?

Historical material lists 10,000 Beans = USD 1, but this is unverified. Check the current conversion rate in the official app or through authorised documentation.

Is withdrawal guaranteed?

No. Withdrawals depend on the current rules, including minimum amounts, verification, payment methods and account eligibility.

Can I register through an agency?

Some hosts register through an agency. If you do, make sure the agency is genuine, does not charge you a fee and never asks for your password or codes. Read our agency responsibilities guide to know what to expect.

How can I protect my account?

Use a strong, unique password, never share verification codes, keep personal details private and link only payment accounts in your own name.

Where can I check current payment requirements?

Inside the official app, in the platform’s published help or policy pages, or through its official support channel.
