Overview

How Manager Host Earnings Work

Manager Hosts earn coins through calls, streams, and gifts. Coins are converted to USD and paid weekly.

🪙
01. Coins Per Minute

Level-Based Coin Rate

Hosts earn coins based on their level during every minute of a video call. D Level earns 40 coins/min while SS Level earns 150 coins/min. The higher your level, the more you earn per minute of call time.

💱
02. Exchange Rate

Coins to USD Conversion

Coins convert to USD at approximately 750–850 coins per $1. Weekly earnings are calculated every Wednesday and the USD amount is transferred to your bank account or e-pay wallet.

🎁
03. Gift Income

Virtual Gift Earnings

Users send virtual gifts during calls and streams as appreciation. These gifts convert directly to coins and are added to your weekly earnings balance alongside your call income.

Tips

How to Increase Your Manager Host Earnings

Proven strategies to maximize your weekly income as a Manager Host.

01

Level Up

Advance from D to C to B and higher levels. An SS Level host earns 150 coins/min vs 40 coins/min at D Level — that's 3.75x more income for the same call time. Leveling up is the highest-impact action.

02

Stay Active Daily

Daily activity unlocks bonuses and improves your platform ranking. Hosts who are consistently active every day earn significantly more than irregular hosts at the same level.

03

Engage Your Users

Keep conversations interesting, friendly, and engaging. Users who enjoy talking with you return more often and send more virtual gifts, directly increasing your weekly coin balance.

04

Go Live Regularly

Regular live streaming sessions build your fan base and allow you to earn from multiple users at once. Live streams are a powerful multiplier for your weekly income above private calls.
