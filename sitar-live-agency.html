Sitar Live Agency Registration in India

Start Your Own Sitar Live Agency

A Sitar Live agency recruits, trains, manages and supports eligible hosts. Agency owners can build their own host-recruitment teams and may receive commission according to the eligible weekly income generated by their hosts. Starting a Sitar Live agency may be suitable for people with experience in online recruitment, team management, social-media promotion, host training, video-calling applications, live-streaming platforms and customer support.

Earnings

Sitar Live Agency Commission

The stated agency commission is based on the total eligible weekly income generated by the agency's host team.

Weekly Host-Team Income Agency Commission
$0 – $799 15%
$800 – $1,499 20%
$1,500 or more 25%
Commission example: When an agency's eligible host team generates $1,000 during a week, the stated commission rate may be 20%. Commission rates, eligibility rules and calculation methods may change according to current platform policies.
Growth

Sitar Live Sub-Agency Programme

Eligible agents may be allowed to recruit and manage sub-agencies.

Weekly Team Income Sub-Agent Commission Senior-Agent Commission
$0 – $799 10% 5%
$800 – $1,499 12% 8%
$1,500 or more 15% 10%
Example: When a sub-agency generates $1,000 in eligible weekly income, the sub-agent may receive 12% (equal to $120) and the senior agent may receive 8% (equal to $80).
The stated policy requires a business-development agent to recruit at least five valid sub-agencies. Each valid sub-agency should have at least ten active hosts to remain eligible for the stated commission structure. Applicants should confirm the latest rules before joining.
Rules

Sitar Live Agency Rules

Agency accounts may be suspended or cancelled for activities such as the following.

🚫 Activities That May Lead to Suspension

  • ✗Registering fake hosts
  • ✗Creating duplicate accounts
  • ✗Encouraging multiple host accounts
  • ✗Withholding host payments
  • ✗Providing false earning information
  • ✗Generating coins through fraudulent methods
  • ✗Manipulating calls or gifts
  • ✗Using unsupported payment channels
  • ✗Promoting competing applications
  • ✗Sharing private host contact details
  • ✗Misusing host documents
  • ✗Allowing unauthorised account access
Agencies must operate honestly and protect the interests of their hosts.
