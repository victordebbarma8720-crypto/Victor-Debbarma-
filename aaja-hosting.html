Earning Methods

How Hosts Earn

Three main ways to earn diamonds and increase your weekly income as an Aaja Host.

📹
01. Video Calls

Private Video Calls

Earn diamonds based on call duration and viewer engagement. The longer and more engaging the call, the more diamonds you receive. Active hosts earn significantly more through consistent call time.

🔀
02. Match Calls

Match Calls

Aaja automatically connects hosts with viewers through match calls. This gives you a steady stream of new connections and earning opportunities without waiting for direct calls.

🎁
03. Virtual Gifts

Virtual Gifts

Viewers can send virtual gifts during conversations to show appreciation. These gifts convert to diamonds, which are calculated as part of your weekly earnings and withdrawal balance.

Working Tips

Tips to Earn More as a Host

Simple habits that help successful Aaja Hosts grow their weekly income.

  • Go live consistently: Regular, predictable hours help you build a returning audience.
  • Use good lighting: Clear, well-lit video improves call quality and viewer retention.
  • Stay engaging: Active conversation keeps callers on longer, increasing diamonds earned.
  • Complete daily tasks: Daily and weekly tasks add reliable bonus income on top of calls.
  • Maintain a stable connection: Dropped calls hurt both earnings and your host rating.
  • Follow platform guidelines: Staying compliant protects your account and long-term income.
