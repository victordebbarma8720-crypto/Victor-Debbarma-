A Sitarlive Agency recruits, assists and manages eligible hosts who work on the Sitarlive App.

What Is a Sitarlive Agency?

A Sitarlive Agency works as a recruitment and host-management partner.

The agency may help applicants complete registration, understand platform rules, begin onboarding and maintain policy-compliant activity.

Agency earnings are performance-based and are not a fixed salary.

Benefits of Becoming an Agency

  • Build your own host recruitment team
  • Recruit eligible hosts online
  • Earn performance-based commission
  • Increase your commission tier through team growth
  • Create eligible sub-agencies where permitted
  • Develop a wider recruitment network
  • Receive onboarding and policy guidance
  • Get WhatsApp support

Agency Responsibilities

  • Recruit genuine and eligible applicants
  • Explain registration requirements clearly
  • Avoid false income promises
  • Support hosts during onboarding
  • Explain platform rules accurately
  • Encourage professional behaviour
  • Avoid fake accounts and manipulated activity
  • Protect host information
  • Follow all applicable platform policies

Sub-Agency Information

Eligible agencies may be permitted to create or manage sub-agencies.

Sub-agency availability, eligibility, access and commission arrangements depend on current platform rules.
