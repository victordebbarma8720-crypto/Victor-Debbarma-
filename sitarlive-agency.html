Earnings

Agent Commission Structure

Your commission rate is based on your hosts' weekly total gift and call income, as defined in the official Sitarlive Agent Working Policy.

Weekly Total Gift & Call Income of Hosts Agent Commission
$0 – $799 15%
$800 – $1,499 20%
$1,500 and above 25%
How it works: Your agent commission is calculated as a percentage of the total weekly gift and call income generated by all the hosts in your team. The more your hosts earn in a week, the higher percentage tier your entire team qualifies for.
Sub-Agent Program

Sub-Agent Commission

Recruit sub-agents to grow your network. Both the sub-agent and the referring agent earn commission on the sub-agent's host income.

Weekly Total Gift & Call Income of Hosts Sub-Agent Commission Agent Commission
$0 – $799 10% 5%
$800 – $1,499 12% 8%
$1,500 and above 15% 10%
Example: If your sub-agent's team has a weekly income of $1,000, the sub-agent earns 1,000 × 12% = $120, and you (the referring agent) earn 1,000 × 8% = $80 on top of your own agent commission.
BD Requirement: Every Business Development (BD) agent must invite at least 5 valid sub-agencies (each with 10 or more active hosts) to receive full sub-agent commission. Otherwise, the rate is reduced to 1%, or the BD status may be cancelled.

Sitarlive Team Policy

Regulations & Punishments

The following behaviors are strictly forbidden in Sitarlive. Agent identity will be cancelled if an agency engages in any of these:

🚫 Prohibited Agency Behavior

  • ✗Allowing or encouraging hosts to create multiple accounts
  • ✗Stealing or deducting the salary of hosts
  • ✗Collecting coins fraudulently (fake coins)
  • ✗Asking users for money via unsupported payment methods
  • ✗Sharing links to other apps with users
  • ✗Sharing a host's personal contact info (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) with users

✅ Agency Best Practices

  • ✓One account per host, verified during onboarding
  • ✓Pay hosts their full, accurate earnings on time
  • ✓Report suspicious coin activity to Sitarlive support
  • ✓Keep all transactions within the official Sitarlive App
  • ✓Protect host privacy and platform trust at all times
  • ✓Maintain at least 5 valid sub-agencies for full BD commission
