Introduction

What Is a Sitarlive Agency?

An overview of what the agency role involves on the Sitarlive platform.

A Sitarlive agency partner typically recruits and supports hosts, helping them get onboarded onto the Sitarlive app and stay active on the platform. In return, agencies can earn commission based on the performance of the hosts they manage.

Sitarlive Agency India offers independent guidance to people exploring this path — helping you understand what's generally expected of an agency partner and how to begin the Sitarlive agency registration process through our WhatsApp support team.
