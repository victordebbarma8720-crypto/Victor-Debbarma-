What Is a Sitarlive Agency?

A Sitarlive Agency works as a recruitment and host-management partner.

The agency may help applicants complete registration, understand platform rules, begin onboarding and maintain policy-compliant activity.

Agency earnings are performance-based and are not a fixed salary.

FAQ

Agency FAQ

Is agency approval guaranteed?

No. Agency approval depends on the information submitted, eligibility and current platform review.

Is agency income fixed?

No. Agency income is performance-based and depends on eligible weekly host activity and current commission tiers.

Can an agency create sub-agencies?

Eligible agencies may be permitted to create sub-agencies, subject to current platform rules and approval.

How is commission calculated?

Commission is calculated as a percentage of eligible weekly host income, according to the applicable commission tier.

Can I recruit hosts from other countries?

This depends on the applicable platform policy at the time of recruitment. Please confirm current eligibility with our support team.

Do I need previous agency experience?

Previous experience is not always required, but the ability to recruit and support a team responsibly is generally helpful.
