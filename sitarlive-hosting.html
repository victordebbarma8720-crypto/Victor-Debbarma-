Guidelines

Working Rules & Prohibited Activities

Please review these standards before registering as a Sitarlive host.

✅ Working Rules

  • ✓Be respectful and professional at all times
  • ✓Maintain proper appearance and lighting during calls
  • ✓Stay active and responsive to incoming calls
  • ✓Follow all current Sitarlive app guidelines
  • ✓Report technical or user issues to your agency or support

🚫 Prohibited Activities

  • ✗Sharing personal contact information with users
  • ✗Conducting private transactions outside the app
  • ✗Misleading users to solicit gifts
  • ✗Operating multiple unauthorized accounts
  • ✗Allowing minors to appear on calls
Warning Policy: Violations of working rules or prohibited activities are generally addressed through platform warnings, temporary restrictions, or account action, depending on severity, as determined by the Sitarlive app's own policies. Our agency team can help clarify these policies, but enforcement decisions are made by the platform itself.
