What Is Sitarlive Hosting?

Sitarlive Hosting means working as an eligible host on the Sitarlive App.

Hosts may interact with users and receive coins through available platform features. Earnings depend on host level, eligible activity, user engagement and current rules.

Earning Features

How Hosts May Earn

Private Video Calls

Eligible hosts may earn coins per minute during one-to-one private video calls. The applicable rate depends on the host's current level.

Match Calls

An eligible match call lasting 30 seconds may reward 100 coins, subject to platform validation and current rules.

Virtual Gifts

Users may send eligible virtual gifts during interactions. Gift value may contribute to the host's platform income.

Performance Rewards

Qualifying hosts may receive ranking, promotional or performance-based rewards when available.

FAQ

Hosting FAQ

Is host approval guaranteed?

No. Host approval depends on the submitted information, eligibility and current platform review.

How much can a host earn?

There is no fixed earning amount. Income depends on eligible activity, host level, call duration, user engagement and platform policies.

Can the call rate increase?

Yes, eligible hosts may increase their private call rate by progressing through host levels based on required income.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

The current policy lists a minimum withdrawal of $10, subject to change under applicable platform rules.

How do I download the Sitarlive App?

Use only a verified Sitarlive App download link provided during registration. Avoid unknown or modified APK files.

Can I use another person's photos?

No. Hosts should use genuine profile photographs and accurate information at all times.

When will my withdrawal arrive?

Eligible withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, subject to current platform processing times.
