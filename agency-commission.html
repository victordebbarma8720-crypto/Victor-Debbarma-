Overview

How Manager Agency Commission Works

Three key things to understand about the Manager Agency commission system.

📊
01. Team-Based

Commission on Team Income

Your commission is calculated on the total weekly income earned by ALL hosts in your team combined. The more hosts you have and the more they earn, the higher your agency's total commission payment.

📈
02. Tiered Rates

Automatic Rate Progression

Commission rates start at 10% and automatically advance to higher tiers as your team's weekly income grows. You don't need to apply for rate upgrades — the system calculates your rate based on performance.

💳
03. Weekly Payouts

Every Wednesday Payment

Commissions are calculated every Wednesday after the Monday–Sunday settlement period closes. Payments are transferred the same day via bank transfer or e-pay with no processing fee on bank transfers.

Additional Income

Sub-Agency Bonus

Recruit sub-agents under your agency for additional rewards beyond your standard commission.

🔗

What Are Sub-Agents?

Sub-agents are other agencies that you recruit and mentor under your umbrella agency. You earn additional bonus rewards based on their team's performance, separate from your own host commissions.

💰

Additional Income Stream

Sub-agency bonuses represent a second income stream on top of your direct host team commission. Experienced agencies use sub-agents to multiply their weekly earnings significantly.

🏗️

Build a Network

Recruiting sub-agents allows you to build a wider Manager Agency network and multiply your earning potential well beyond your direct host team capacity.

🎯

Growth Strategy

The most successful Manager agencies combine strong host team management with strategic sub-agency recruitment for maximum weekly earnings and long-term growth.

Growth Tips

How to Maximize Your Agency Commission

Proven strategies to grow your team income and unlock higher commission tiers.

01

Recruit Actively

The more active hosts in your team, the higher your team's total weekly income and commission tier. Consistent, ongoing recruitment is the most important activity for agency growth.

02

Help Hosts Level Up

An SS Level host earns 150 coins/min vs 40 coins/min at D Level — 3.75x more. Helping your hosts advance their levels directly multiplies their weekly earnings and your commission rate.

03

Retain Top Performers

Keep your best hosts engaged and motivated. Top-performing hosts who stay active long-term are the foundation of a high-earning agency. Support them and recognize their contributions.

04

Develop Sub-Agents

Once your agency is established, recruit and mentor sub-agents for additional bonus income on top of your standard commission. Sub-agent bonuses compound your total weekly earnings.
