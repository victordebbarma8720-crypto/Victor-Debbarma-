What is a Cherry Agency?

Recruit, Manage & Earn

A Cherry Agency is a business partner who recruits talent (Hosts) onto the Cherry live platform and earns a percentage of their total monthly diamond earnings converted to USD.

Your role is to find hosts, support their growth, help them stay active, and watch your commission climb up the tiers. The more your hosts collectively earn, the higher your commission class — and the more you take home every week.

Cherry Agency India provides full support to help you get registered, understand the rules, and grow your team effectively.

Commission Base

Earned from total monthly host earnings converted to USD via 10,000 diamonds = $1 rate.

Calculation Day

Every Monday at 07:30 IST (UTC+5:30). Commission auto-calculated by platform.

FAQ

Agency Questions Answered

How is my agency commission calculated?

Commission = Total host diamond earnings ÷ 10,000 × your commission rate %. For example, if your hosts earn 5,000,000 diamonds in a month that equals $500. At Class C (20%), you receive $100. The calculation runs automatically every Monday at 07:30 IST.

How many hosts do I need to reach Class F?

There is no set number. Class F requires your total agency to generate ≥$10,000 in monthly USD earnings. This could be 5 very active hosts or 50 moderately active ones. Focus on quality, consistency and training your hosts to perform well.

Can I also be a Host and an Agency?

Typically, Cherry does not allow one person to hold both a Host and Agency account simultaneously. Please consult with our support team on WhatsApp to clarify the latest platform policy on this.

What happens if my hosts are inactive?

If your hosts earn less than the Class A threshold ($300/month), you will earn no commission for that period. Inactive hosts do not earn diamonds, so keeping your team motivated and consistent is key to maintaining your commission class.
