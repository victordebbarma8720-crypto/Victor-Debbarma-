How Sitarlive Hosts May Earn

Multiple Earning Features for Eligible Hosts

📹

Private Video Calls

Eligible hosts may earn coins per minute during one-to-one private video calls. The applicable rate depends on the host's current level.

🤝

Match Calls

An eligible match call lasting 30 seconds may reward 100 coins, subject to platform validation and current rules.

🎁

Virtual Gifts

Users may send eligible virtual gifts during interactions. Gift value may contribute to the host's platform income.

🏆

Performance Rewards

Qualifying hosts may receive ranking, promotional or performance-based rewards when available.

SitarliveAgency.in does not guarantee fixed income, call traffic, virtual gifts or account performance. Results depend on eligible activity, host level, user engagement, policy compliance and platform conditions.
