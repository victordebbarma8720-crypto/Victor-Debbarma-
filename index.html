FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Masti host registration, Masti App, Masti Anchor Agency, and earnings.

What is Mastianchors.com?+
Mastianchors.com is an independent information and onboarding guidance website. We are not the official Masti platform. Our website helps people understand how to join Masti as a host or start a Masti Agency in India, including details on registration, earnings, withdrawal, and weekly tasks.
Is there any fee to register as a Masti host?+
No. Masti host registration is free. You only need to download the Masti App and register. Be cautious of anyone claiming to charge a registration fee, as official registration is completely free.
How much can a Masti host earn per month?+
Earnings depend on your working hours, the number of video calls, gifts received, and match earnings. A dedicated Masti host who follows the weekly task schedule and works during peak hours (9 PM–1 AM IST) can earn a significant income. Beans are converted at 10,000 Beans = $1 USD.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount on Masti?+
The minimum withdrawal on Masti is $5 USD. Withdrawals must be in multiples of $5. Daily withdrawals are supported, and self-withdrawal is allowed directly through the Masti App.
Can one person have multiple Masti accounts?+
No. The Masti platform strictly allows only one account per person. Operating multiple accounts is a violation of Masti's terms and can result in permanent account suspension.
What is the Masti Weekly Bonus?+
Masti rewards hosts who complete their weekly tasks. S Level hosts receive a $15 bonus and their agency earns $3. A Level hosts receive a $10 bonus and their agency earns $2. Weekly tasks include 5 valid working days, 120 minutes of daily peak hours, and maintaining call answer rates above 90%.
