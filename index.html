FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Cherry Agency India.

What is Cherry Agency India?

Cherry Agency India is an authorised registration and support partner for the Cherry live entertainment platform. We help talent across India register as Hosts or Agencies, guiding them through the setup and earning process.

How much can I earn as a Host?

Hosts earn 1,000 diamonds per minute on 1-on-1 video calls, 70% of all virtual gift diamonds, and 500 diamonds per minute in Match mode. 10,000 diamonds = $1 USD. Minimum withdrawal is $10, processed in 3 working days (UTC+8).

What is the agency commission structure?

There are 6 commission classes: Class A (≥$300/month = 15%), B (≥$600 = 18%), C (≥$1,000 = 20%), D (≥$2,000 = 25%), E (≥$5,000 = 35%), F (≥$10,000 = 40%). Commission is calculated every Monday at 07:30 IST.

What is the sub-agent commission?

If you invite sub-agents under your agency, you earn 10% of their commission — but only when the sub-agent's hosts earn at least $10 in that week.

Is registration free?

Yes, registration is completely free for both Hosts and Agencies. We never charge any upfront fees. Our WhatsApp support team will help you complete the process at no cost.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Withdrawals are requested through the Cherry app. The minimum withdrawal is $10. Reviews take 3 working days (UTC+8 timezone). Make sure your bank details are correctly linked in your Cherry profile.
