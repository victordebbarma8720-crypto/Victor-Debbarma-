Registration

How Does Chamet Agency Registration Work?

The general steps below give an overview. Exact requirements depend on the current program.

  1. Review eligibility

    Check the current eligibility requirements before you apply.

  2. Verify the channel

    Confirm that the registration channel is genuinely authorised.

  3. Submit accurate information

    Provide complete, truthful application details.

  4. Complete verification

    Finish any identity or account verification that is required.

  5. Follow onboarding

    Use the authorised onboarding procedure you are given.

  6. Learn the system

    If approved, learn to use the authorised management system.

  7. Understand commission

    Know how commission is calculated and what you are responsible for.

Please note: applying does not guarantee approval or income. Decisions are made under the applicable platform program.

Host recruitment

Recruit and Support Eligible Chamet Hosts

Responsible recruitment protects hosts and agencies alike. Good practice includes:

📋

Accurate information

Share correct, current registration information and authorised channels only.

📖

Explain the guidelines

Walk hosts through the applicable hosting guidelines and content rules.

📷

Profile requirements

Help hosts understand what a suitable, rule-compliant profile looks like.

🔒

Respect privacy

Never ask for passwords or codes, and keep hosts’ personal details private.

⚖️

No misleading promises

Avoid earnings promises — income depends on eligible activity and platform rules.

✅

Follow platform rules

Follow the applicable platform terms and agency agreement at all times.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Chamet

What is a Chamet agency?

A Chamet agency arrangement may allow eligible partners to recruit or manage hosts under the applicable platform program. The precise responsibilities depend on current program terms. See the agency guide.

How can I apply for Chamet agency registration?

Review the agency registration guide, check the current eligibility requirements, and apply only through a registration channel you have independently verified as authorised.

How can I register as a Chamet host?

Review the host registration guide and follow the current authorised registration procedure, including any required verification.

How much can a Chamet host earn?

Earnings vary according to eligible activity and applicable platform policies. No specific income can be guaranteed.

Are agency commissions guaranteed?

No. Commission eligibility and payments depend on the applicable program rules and eligible activity.
