FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sitarlive Agency and Hosting programs.

Sitarlive Agency is a partner program where agencies recruit hosts, build teams, and earn commissions based on host performance, as defined in the official Sitarlive Agent Working Policy. Agencies receive weekly payouts every Sunday.
You can complete Sitarlive Agency Registration by contacting our support team on WhatsApp at +91 8132958338. Our team will guide you through the onboarding process step by step.
Agent commission is based on your hosts' weekly total gift and call income: 15% for $0–799, 20% for $800–1,499, and 25% for $1,500 and above. Sub-agent recruitment unlocks additional commission on top of your own agent rate.
To start Sitarlive Hosting, contact our WhatsApp support at +91 8132958338 and complete the onboarding process. Eligible hosts receive an account and can begin earning coins through live streaming, calls, and virtual gifts.
Contact our support team for Sitarlive App Download guidance and registration support. Our team will provide step-by-step assistance to ensure you get started quickly and correctly.
Withdrawals are processed every Sunday (UTC+0) via Bank Transfer or USDT (TRC20), with a $10 minimum withdrawal. Payments complete within three working days of the withdrawal request. Bank withdrawals carry a 5% fee, and USDT withdrawals carry a fee of more than $2.
