About Us

About Sitar Live Agency India

Sitar Live Agency India is an independent registration and onboarding support platform. We assist applicants interested in joining Sitar Live as hosts, agents, agency owners or sub-agency partners. Our goal is to simplify the application process, explain eligibility and working requirements, reduce registration mistakes and provide clear policy guidance.

What We May Assist With

Host registration, agency registration, profile verification, app installation, host training, payment information, commission explanations and account-related guidance.

What We Don't Control

Account approval, host level, call traffic, payment processing, commission calculation, account suspension, platform features or policy updates — all final decisions are controlled by Sitar Live and its authorised management.
