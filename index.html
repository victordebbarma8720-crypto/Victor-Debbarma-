Agency Opportunity

Build Your Own Aaja Agency

An Aaja Agency recruits, trains, and supports a team of hosts — earning commission on the team's performance.

Starting an Aaja Agency is a long-term opportunity for people who enjoy recruiting, mentoring, and managing others. As an agency, your role is to find suitable candidates, guide them through Aaja Host Registration, help them understand daily and weekly tasks, and support them as they grow through the host level system. In return, your agency earns a commission based on your team's combined weekly performance.

A successful Aaja Agency is built on consistency — steady recruitment, active communication with hosts, and helping your team stay engaged with daily tasks and weekly rewards. As your team grows and becomes more active, your agency can unlock higher commission tiers and even develop sub-agencies for additional growth.
