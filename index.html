What is Chingari?

Chingari is one of India's leading short-video, live streaming, and social entertainment platforms. Launched in 2018 and rising rapidly, Chingari has become a major destination for digital creators, live hosts, and audiences across India.

The platform enables content creators to share short videos, host live rooms, build communities, and connect with millions of users. Chingari supports Indian languages, making it deeply accessible across urban and rural India.

With its growing creator economy, Chingari offers meaningful opportunities for hosts, agencies, and creators who want to build a presence in the Indian social entertainment space.
