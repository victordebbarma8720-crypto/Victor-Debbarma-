About Kamaichat

What Is Kamaichat?

Kamaichat is a video chat and communication platform where eligible hosts can participate in video calls, messaging and qualifying platform activities.

Depending on the applicable platform rules, eligible hosts may receive rewards for qualifying interactions and tasks.

Our website provides information and registration assistance for people interested in Kamaichat host and agency opportunities.

Kamaichat Agency

Become a Kamaichat Agency

A Kamaichat agency can recruit and support eligible hosts and may receive commissions according to the applicable agency arrangement.

Agencies may assist hosts with registration, onboarding, platform guidance and ongoing support.

Join Us Agency

Agency Responsibilities

  • Recruit eligible hosts
  • Assist with host registration
  • Support new hosts during onboarding
  • Explain applicable platform requirements
  • Support active hosts
  • Build and manage a host network

Eligibility

Kamaichat Host Requirements

The provided recruitment information is intended for eligible female applicants aged 18 or above who are comfortable communicating with users.

  • At least 18 years of age
  • Good communication skills
  • Android or iPhone smartphone
  • Stable internet connection
  • Valid payment details
  • Suitable profile and cover photo
  • Regular availability
All applicants remain subject to applicable platform requirements and approval procedures.
