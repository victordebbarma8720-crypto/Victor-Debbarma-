Earning Features

How Sitarlive Hosts May Earn

Multiple Earning Features for Eligible Hosts

Private Video Calls

Eligible hosts may earn coins per minute during one-to-one private video calls. The applicable rate depends on the host's current level.

Match Calls

An eligible match call lasting 30 seconds may reward 100 coins, subject to platform validation and current rules.

Virtual Gifts

Users may send eligible virtual gifts during interactions. Gift value may contribute to the host's platform income.

Performance Rewards

Qualifying hosts may receive ranking, promotional or performance-based rewards when available.

SitarliveAgency.in does not guarantee fixed income, call traffic, virtual gifts or account performance. Results depend on eligible activity, host level, user engagement, policy compliance and platform conditions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sitarlive?

Sitarlive is a social and video communication platform where eligible hosts may interact with users through available calling and gift features.

What is a Sitarlive Agency?

A Sitarlive Agency recruits and supports eligible hosts and may receive performance-based commission according to the applicable agency policy.

How can I complete Sitarlive host registration?

Open the Sitarlive Hosting page, review the available information and contact our support team for registration assistance.

How can I apply for a Sitarlive Agency?

Open the Sitarlive Agency page and follow the available application process.

How do Sitarlive hosts earn?

Eligible hosts may receive coins through private video calls, match calls, virtual gifts and applicable performance rewards.

How much can a Sitarlive host earn?

There is no fixed earning amount. Income depends on eligible activity, host level, call duration, user engagement and platform policies.

Is Sitarlive registration free?

Any applicable charges should be confirmed clearly before registration. Do not send money to unknown people or unverified accounts.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The supplied policy information lists the minimum withdrawal as $10, subject to the current platform policy.

How can I download the Sitarlive App?

Use only a verified Sitarlive App download link provided during registration. Avoid unknown or modified APK files.

How long does approval take?

Approval time may vary depending on verification, submitted information, platform workload and eligibility.

Does SitarliveAgency.in guarantee approval or income?

No. We provide independent registration and onboarding assistance. Final approval, income, traffic and payments depend on the platform.
