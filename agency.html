Responsibilities

What Does a Sitarlive Agency Do?

As an agency partner, you play an active role in helping hosts succeed on the platform.

  • Recruit new hosts and guide them through registration
  • Support hosts with tips on going live and growing an audience
  • Monitor team activity and encourage consistent engagement
  • Act as the first point of contact for your hosts' questions

Team Building

  • Identify potential hosts within your network
  • Help them download the app and register with Agency Code C063
  • Provide ongoing motivation and mentorship
  • Grow your agency steadily with quality over quantity
