🏢 About Agency Program

What is the Chingari Agency Program?

The Chingari Agency Program allows entrepreneurs and business-minded individuals to build, recruit, and manage a team of Chingari creators and live hosts — creating a scalable presence in India's rapidly growing creator economy.

As a Chingari Agency Partner, you act as a talent management entity on the platform. You recruit hosts, support their growth, and benefit from your team's collective performance and community engagement.

Our platform provides free agency registration support, step-by-step onboarding, and ongoing WhatsApp guidance to help you establish and grow your Chingari Agency efficiently.

❓ FAQ

Agency Registration FAQ

Common questions about Chingari agency registration and the agency partner program.

A Chingari Agency recruits and manages a team of live hosts and creators on the platform. The agency is responsible for onboarding hosts, supporting their growth, monitoring their performance, and ensuring they follow community guidelines. Agencies play a vital role in expanding Chingari's creator ecosystem.
There is no fixed cap on the number of hosts an agency can manage. Agencies can start small (1–5 hosts) and scale up to dozens or hundreds of creators as the agency grows. Our team will advise you on recommended team sizes during onboarding.
Yes, many agency partners start part-time while managing other commitments. As your agency grows, you may choose to dedicate more time to it. The Chingari Agency program does not impose fixed working hours on agency partners.
No, you do not need to have a full team ready before applying. You can start the agency registration process as an individual and begin recruiting hosts as part of your onboarding. Our team will guide you on the recruitment process during agency training.
A Chingari Host is an individual creator who goes live on the platform. A Chingari Agency is an entity that recruits and manages multiple hosts. Agency partners act as talent managers — supporting, guiding, and growing their team of hosts — rather than hosting themselves directly.
