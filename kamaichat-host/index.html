Eligibility

Kamaichat Host Requirements

  • Minimum age of 18
  • Good communication skills
  • Android or iPhone smartphone
  • Stable internet connection
  • Valid payment details
  • Suitable profile and cover photo
  • Regular availability
The recruitment information provided to us is for eligible female applicants aged 18 or above.
