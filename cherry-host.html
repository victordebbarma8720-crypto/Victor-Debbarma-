FAQ

Host Questions Answered

How much can I earn per day as a Cherry Host?

Earnings vary by activity level. As an example: 2 hours of video calls at 1,000 diamonds/min = 120,000 diamonds = $12. Add virtual gift earnings on top. Active hosts with engaged audiences earn significantly more. Top hosts earn hundreds of dollars per week.

How long does it take to withdraw my earnings?

Withdrawal requests are reviewed within 3 working days (UTC+8 timezone). Funds are transferred directly to your linked bank account. The minimum withdrawal amount is $10. Ensure your bank account details are correctly entered to avoid delays.

Do I need any special equipment to go live?

All you need is a smartphone with a decent camera, stable internet (4G or WiFi), and a reasonably quiet and well-lit environment. No professional studio equipment is required to get started. Many successful hosts use just their phone.

Can I set my own streaming hours?

Yes, you are fully flexible. You choose when and how long to stream. However, streaming at consistent times and for longer sessions generally results in higher earnings as audiences learn your schedule and return regularly.

What happens to the 30% of gift diamonds I don't receive?

The remaining 30% of virtual gift diamonds goes to the platform (Cherry) and your registered agency. Your agency earns their commission percentage from this pool. As a host, you always keep 70% of all virtual gifts — that is your guaranteed share.
